** Identity & forms
- 17α-estradiol (alfatradiol) is a stereoisomer distinct from 17β-estradiol; topical scalp solutions are marketed in some countries. “Transdermal” preparations and compounded systemic products are not interchangeable. [PMID:22879713]
** Mechanisms
- Proposed local effects include inhibition of 5α-reductase and altered androgen metabolism; receptor potency and systemic estrogenic action differ from 17β-estradiol. [PMID:22879713]

** Dosing in trials
- Study used topical scalp 0.025% solution once daily for 8 months [PMID:22879713]. [PMID:22879713]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Local itching/irritation; systemic exposure and reproductive/endocrine safety depend on formulation/site/dose and are inadequately characterized. [PMID:22879713]
** Contraindications
- Avoid use in pregnancy; systemic estrogen-sensitive conditions require clinician evaluation. No established safety for compounded systemic/transdermal use. [PMID:22879713]
** Interactions
- No controlled interaction studies; potential additive hormonal effects with other estrogenic/antiandrogenic drugs are theoretical. [PMID:22879713]
** Genotype notes
- No genotype evidence identified.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: No high-quality recent controlled trial identified in the last 24 months. [PMID:22879713]
** Open questions
- Systemic absorption, efficacy by sex, and whether topical findings translate to transdermal systemic delivery are unknown. [PMID:22879713]
