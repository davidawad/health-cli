** Identity & forms
- Acetyl-L-carnitine is the acetylated form of L-carnitine, an endogenous mitochondrial fatty-acid transport compound; oral capsules and pharmaceutical preparations have been studied [PMID:29076953].

** Mechanisms
- Proposed roles include mitochondrial fatty-acid transport, acetyl-group donation, and neurotrophic/neurotransmission effects; mechanistic findings do not establish efficacy [PMID:30061399].

** Dosing in trials
- Depression trials used heterogeneous oral regimens, often 1–3 g/day in divided doses; the meta-analysis did not establish an optimal dose [PMID:29076953].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Trials generally reported tolerability, but adverse-event ascertainment and sample sizes were limited; nausea, agitation, and odor-related effects are described in clinical use literature [PMID:29076953].

** Contraindications
- Caution in seizure disorders is identified in NIH ODS carnitine safety information; pregnancy safety is not established [https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Carnitine-Consumer/].

** Interactions
- Drugs, supplements, conditions, and relevant laboratory interference are summarized in the structured interaction object. A theoretical coumarin-anticoagulant interaction is described for L-carnitine, while direct evidence for acetyl-L-carnitine is lacking [https://www.blackmoresinstitute.org/-/media/bklbi/pdf/cmig_11th_edition.pdf]. No genotype-specific claim is made because no directly relevant pharmacogenomic source was identified.

** Genotype notes
- No directly relevant, clinically validated pharmacogenomic/nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed.

** Latest research
- 2026: systematic review/meta-analysis included 14 RCTs and one open-label study (809 participants), reporting lower depressive symptom scores but high heterogeneity and serious-to-critical bias risk [PMID:42261369].

** Open questions
- Large preregistered trials with low risk of bias, standardized dose, and functional outcomes are needed; latest pooled effects are highly heterogeneous [PMID:42261369].
