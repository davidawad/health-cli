** Identity & forms
- Acetyl-L-carnitine (ALC, ALCAR, LAC) is the acetyl ester of L-carnitine and the most abundant ester in the body's carnitine pool, both quantitatively and functionally [PMID:15591001].
- Forms used in trials: Oral capsules or tablets of acetyl-L-carnitine (usually the hydrochloride), most often 1-3 g/day [PMID:8797468] [PMID:29076953]. Intramuscular injection followed by oral dosing, in HIV neuropathy and fibromyalgia trials [PMID:17461852] [PMID:17543140].
- Pharmaceutical status: in Italy it is sold as a pharmaceutical product (Nicetile, Sigma-Tau group), including an injectable form used for neuropathies, diabetic polyneuropathy and Parkinson's disease [PMID:20661677]. In the US it is sold as a dietary supplement. The related drug levocarnitine (Carnitor) is a US prescription product, and its label is the main regulatory safety source for the carnitine family [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].
- Pharmacokinetics: Oral L-carnitine supplements (0.5-6 g) are absorbed mostly passively, with 14-18% bioavailability. Acetyl-L-carnitine is partly hydrolyzed in the gut wall, but 2 g/day raised circulating ALC by 43% [PMID:15591001]. Renal reabsorption saturates, so clearance rises and plasma levels fall quickly back to baseline after high doses [PMID:15591001]. In a 2025 human study, ALC gave a 7.7-fold lower plasma exposure increase (delta AUC) than L-carnitine. Most of it was eliminated as carnitine, and about 90% of both supplements was metabolized to TMAO [PMID:41243468].

** Mechanisms
- Carnitine shuttle: moves long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for beta-oxidation. ALC also donates acetyl groups and has neurotrophic effects in peripheral nerve [PMID:31201734] [PMID:41824218].
- Epigenetic and glutamatergic effects (preclinical): In rodent depression models, LAC acetylated histone H3K27 at the Grm2 promoter and raised mGlu2 receptor expression. It produced antidepressant-like effects within 3 days, versus 14 days for chlorimipramine [PMID:23382250]. The 2026 narrative review describes ALC as an epigenetic regulator of mGlu2 receptors and nerve growth factor, which is the proposed basis for its analgesic action [PMID:41824218].
- Depression biomarker: blood ALC levels, but not free carnitine, were lower in patients with major depression at two independent centers. The deficit was larger in more severe, earlier-onset and treatment-resistant depression [PMID:30061399].
- Ammonia lowering: ALC lowered blood ammonia in hepatic encephalopathy trials (Cochrane MA) [PMID:30610762].
- Thyroid hormone antagonism: L-carnitine inhibits T3 and T4 entry into the nuclei of hepatocytes, neurons and fibroblasts. It acts as a peripheral antagonist of thyroid hormone action [PMID:15591013] [PMID:11502782]. In vitro, ALC itself complexed iodine and inhibited lactoperoxidase [PMID:38460232].
- Gut-microbial TMAO pathway: Gut bacteria convert dietary L-carnitine to TMA and then TMAO, which accelerated atherosclerosis in mice [PMID:23563705]. In humans the route runs through gamma-butyrobetaine. The second step is induced by omnivorous diets and by chronic L-carnitine intake [PMID:30530985]. Microbial gbu genes predict how much TMAO a person makes after a carnitine challenge [PMID:39722590].
- Pivalate scavenging: carnitine binds pivalate from pivalate-prodrug antibiotics as pivaloylcarnitine, which is excreted in urine and depletes body carnitine [PMID:12709724].

** Dosing in trials
- Depression: usually 1-3 g/day orally. ALCAR 500 mg twice daily was compared with amisulpride for 12 weeks [PMID:16316746]. No optimal dose has been established [PMID:29076953].
- Alzheimer's disease: 1 g three times daily (3 g/day) for 12 months [PMID:8797468] [PMID:10994000]. The MCI/AD meta-analysis covered 1.5-3.0 g/day for 3-12 months [PMID:12598816].
- Diabetic neuropathy: 1500-3000 mg/day for 6-12 months. Only doses above 1500 mg/day separated from placebo, in a very low-certainty subgroup [PMID:31201734]. 500 mg three times daily was used against methylcobalamin [PMID:27180954].
- CIPN prevention (not recommended): 1000 mg three times daily for 24 weeks during taxane chemotherapy [PMID:29361042].
- HIV neuropathy: 500 mg intramuscular twice daily for 14 days, then 1000 mg oral twice daily [PMID:17461852]. Open-label oral dosing was 1500 mg twice daily for up to 33 months [PMID:15238773].
- Hepatic encephalopathy: 2 g twice daily for 90 days [PMID:21310833] [PMID:21870121].
- Male infertility: L-carnitine 2 g/day plus ALC 1 g/day for 6 months, or ALC 3 g/day alone [PMID:15193480] [PMID:16169400].
- Prefrailty: 1.5 g twice daily for 3 months [PMID:36043711]. Chronic fatigue syndrome: 2 g/day for 24 weeks [PMID:15039515]. ALS: 3 g/day with riluzole for 48 weeks [PMID:23421600].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Short-term tolerability is generally good: In 3 head-to-head RCTs, adverse effects were significantly less frequent with ALC than with antidepressants [PMID:29076953]. The 1-year Alzheimer's trials and the dementia Cochrane review found no significant excess of adverse events over placebo [PMID:8797468] [PMID:10994000] [PMID:12804452].
- Reported AEs include headache, facial paresthesia and gastrointestinal upset. In one DPN trial, 4.1% stopped ALC versus 1.4% on placebo [PMID:31201734]. Neuropathic-pain MAs list pain, headache, paresthesia, hyperesthesia, retching and biliary colic, at rates similar to control [PMID:25751285].
- A fibromyalgia cohort reported AEs in 16.7%, mainly insomnia, shivering, headache and nausea [PMID:40299412].
- In hepatic encephalopathy, non-serious AEs were 6.34% vs 2.50% (RR 2.51, CI 0.68-9.22). Harms reporting was poor, and the review authors say harms may be underestimated [PMID:30610762].
- Worse neuropathy with taxanes: ALC 3 g/day worsened taxane-induced neuropathy at 24 weeks, and the effect persisted for 2 years [PMID:23733756] [PMID:29361042]. ASCO and the Society for Integrative Oncology discourage its use for CIPN prevention [PMID:32663120] [PMID:28436999].
- TMAO: L-carnitine supplementation raised fasting TMAO tenfold in older women without changing inflammatory or lipid markers over 24 weeks [PMID:30485835] [PMID:32958033]. About 90% of an ALC dose was converted to TMAO, reaching about 50 uM in plasma, a level linked to adverse outcomes in observational data [PMID:41243468]. Plasma carnitine predicted cardiovascular events only when TMAO was also high (n=2,595) [PMID:23563705]. Whether this raises cardiovascular risk in people is unproven.
- Carnitine-class label warnings (levocarnitine, Carnitor) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd]: Gastrointestinal: nausea, vomiting, abdominal cramps and diarrhea. Body odor, which decreasing the dose often reduces. Seizures in patients with or without prior seizures, and more frequent or severe seizures in people with a seizure disorder. Hypersensitivity: rash, urticaria and facial edema. In severe renal impairment or dialysis, TMA and TMAO can accumulate.
- Pregnancy: no adequate human studies. Levocarnitine animal studies showed no fetal harm [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].

** Contraindications
- No absolute contraindications are listed for the carnitine class (levocarnitine label: "none known") [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].
- Avoid during taxane (and possibly other neurotoxic) chemotherapy for CIPN prevention, because RCT evidence shows harm [PMID:23733756] [PMID:32663120].
- Known hypersensitivity to carnitine products [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].
- Caution in seizure disorders (label), in severe renal impairment or dialysis (TMAO accumulation), and in hypothyroidism treated with levothyroxine (see Interactions) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd] [PMID:11502782].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Warfarin and acenocoumarol (coumarins): The levocarnitine label reports INR increases with warfarin and advises monitoring INR after starting carnitine or changing its dose [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd]. Case reports describe L-carnitine potentiating acenocoumarol [PMID:8429297] [PMID:15340883]. These data are for L-carnitine. Data specific to ALC are lacking, but ALC is largely metabolized to carnitine [PMID:41243468].
- Thyroid hormone (levothyroxine) and hyperthyroidism treatment: L-carnitine is a peripheral thyroid hormone antagonist. In an RCT, 2-4 g/day reversed and prevented hyperthyroid symptoms in women on TSH-suppressive L-T4 [PMID:11502782] [PMID:15591013]. In people taking levothyroxine for hypothyroidism, it could in theory blunt hormone action. However, an RCT of L-carnitine 990 mg twice daily in hypothyroid patients on L-T4 improved mental fatigue [PMID:27432821]. Data specific to ALC are in vitro only [PMID:38460232].
- Valproate: Valproate is linked to carnitine deficiency, hyperammonemia and encephalopathy [PMID:34004407]. Levocarnitine is used to treat valproate-induced hyperammonemic encephalopathy, with no adverse events reported in the reviewed literature [PMID:22180549]. A systematic review found no study showing carnitine-induced seizures in patients on valproate [PMID:26889779]. A 2026 poisoning review found no kinetic or survival benefit in a retrospective cohort of valproate toxicity [PMID:41692009]. This is a beneficial or neutral interaction, not a harmful one.
- Pivalate-conjugated antibiotics (e.g. cefditoren pivoxil): These cause secondary carnitine deficiency. Cefditoren pivoxil 400 mg twice daily for 14 days cut plasma carnitine from about 45 to 9 umol/L [PMID:12709724]. In children they can cause hypoglycemia and encephalopathy [PMID:27059912]. Carnitine supplementation prevented the deficiency in patients on valproate [PMID:33714931].
- Taxanes and other neurotoxic chemotherapy: ALC worsened taxane CIPN [PMID:23733756] [PMID:29361042].
- Nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors: ALC levels fall in NRTI-associated neuropathy, and ALC has been studied as treatment without stopping the drugs. No harmful interaction is reported [PMID:15238773] [PMID:16566084].
- Duloxetine and pregabalin: adding PEA plus ALC improved fibromyalgia outcomes, with no reported safety signal [PMID:37378482].
- Antidepressants: ALC has been used alone and as an add-on in depression RCTs, but no formal interaction study exists [PMID:29076953].
*** Other supplements
- CoQ10: carnitine and CoQ10 are often combined for male infertility. NMAs and MAs found each improves sperm parameters but not pregnancy rates. This is overlapping use, not a proven synergy [PMID:40813743] [PMID:34871624].
- N-Acetyl-L-Cysteine (NAC) and Citicholine: reviews propose complementary, possibly synergistic, glutamatergic, redox and mitochondrial effects for NAC plus ALC, with citicoline added for cognitive fatigue. This rests on mechanism and early clinical data [PMID:42136265] [PMID:39506442]. In organophosphate poisoning, a mortality signal was seen when L-carnitine was combined with NAC [PMID:41692009].
- Alpha-lipoic acid (not in the corpus list): combining it with ALC had no antidepressant effect in bipolar depression [PMID:23948785].
- B12 methylcobalamin: an active comparator in DPN with similar efficacy. This is a head-to-head comparison, not an interaction [PMID:27180954].
- No cited interaction data were found for Alpha-GPC or Creatine.
*** Conditions
- Breast or other cancers on taxane chemotherapy: avoid for CIPN prevention [PMID:32663120].
- Seizure disorder: the label reports seizures [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd]. A negative systematic review, though, found no supporting literature in valproate-treated patients [PMID:26889779].
- Severe renal impairment or dialysis: TMA and TMAO accumulate [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].
- Cardiovascular disease and high TMAO: raised TMAO is a mechanistic and observational concern [PMID:23563705] [PMID:41243468].
- Hypothyroidism: possible antagonism of thyroid hormone action [PMID:15591013].
- Pregnancy and lactation: no adequate human data [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd].
*** Lab-test interference
- No assay artifact has been documented. The following are true physiological changes: INR may rise with coumarins [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2133bc3-9c15-48bd-8b16-b8995a6a14cd]. Plasma TMAO rises [PMID:30485835]. Serum carnitine rises [PMID:23733756]. Blood ammonia falls in cirrhosis [PMID:30610762].
- Thyroid markers: in the Benvenga RCT, carnitine prevented hyperthyroid shifts in several biochemical markers, but TSH and urinary hydroxyproline did not respond [PMID:15591013].
- Acylcarnitine profiling: pivaloylcarnitine shows up as C5-acylcarnitine in patients on pivalate antibiotics [PMID:27059912].

** Genotype notes
No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source found. TMAO production from carnitine depends on gut-microbial gbu genes, which is a microbiome rather than a host-genotype factor [PMID:39722590].

** Latest research
- [2026-06] SR/MA of mood disorders (14 RCTs + 1 open-label, n=809): ALC reduced depressive symptoms versus placebo, with efficacy comparable to antidepressants and fewer AEs. Larger benefit was seen in older adults and treatment-resistant depression [PMID:42261369].
- [2026-06] SR of mitochondrial-enhancing nutraceuticals in psychiatry (122 studies, including ALC): overall risk of bias was high and methodological quality low [PMID:42253799].
- [2026-05] Review proposes NAC + ALC + citicoline synergy for neurological and psychiatric disorders [PMID:42136265].
- [2026-03] Narrative review of ALC in peripheral neuropathies: epigenetic mGlu2/NGF mechanism; supports long-term neurotrophic and analgesic use [PMID:41824218].
- [2026-02] SR of L-carnitine/ALC in drug poisonings (19 studies): adjunctive benefit in organophosphate and aluminium phosphide poisoning; no benefit in valproate toxicity cohort [PMID:41692009].
- [2025-11] Human PK study: ALC has 7.7-fold lower plasma exposure than L-carnitine, and about 90% of either was converted to TMAO (about 50 uM) [PMID:41243468].
- [2025-08] Male infertility NMA (16 studies): L-carnitine, CoQ10 and L-carnitine + ALC improved sperm quality; L-carnitine was best for progressive motility [PMID:40813743].
- [2025-05] Male infertility MA (50 RCTs): carnitine improved motility, but no supplement improved pregnancy or live birth; certainty low/very low [PMID:40431450].
- [2025-03] MA of L-carnitine vs NAC monotherapy (7 RCTs): L-carnitine raised sperm concentration, morphology, motility and testosterone [PMID:40350672].
- [2025-03] Retrospective fibromyalgia cohort (n=183): VAS fell from 75.9 to 51.9; 16.7% AEs [PMID:40299412].
- [2025-03] Phase II/III RCT of ALC in ALS (target n=246) is recruiting; primary outcome is self-sufficiency [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06126315].
- [2025-02] SR of CIPN drug RCTs: ALC results inconsistent; duloxetine remains the only recommended agent [PMID:39952863].
- [2024-12] gbu gut-microbial genes determine TMAO production after L-carnitine intake [PMID:39722590].

** Open questions
- Depression: large, preregistered, low-bias RCTs are needed. Existing pooled effects are highly heterogeneous and come from small trials [PMID:29076953] [PMID:42253799].
- Why ALC worsened taxane CIPN, and whether this applies to other neurotoxic agents (for example oxaliplatin) [PMID:29361042].
- Neuropathy: dose-response and durability of benefit in diabetic neuropathy; current trials are manufacturer-funded [PMID:31201734].
- Hepatic encephalopathy: whether ALC improves clinical outcomes, not just ammonia. All trials so far are from a single group [PMID:30610762].
- TMAO: whether the TMAO rise from chronic supplementation affects cardiovascular events in humans [PMID:41243468] [PMID:30485835].
- Anticoagulants and thyroid hormone: whether the L-carnitine coumarin interaction and thyroid antagonism apply at usual ALC doses.
- Long COVID fatigue and neuropsychiatric symptoms: proposed on mechanistic grounds, but no RCT evidence was cited [PMID:38172332] [PMID:39506442].
- ALS: confirmation of the pilot trial in the ongoing phase II/III study [PMID:23421600] [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06126315].
