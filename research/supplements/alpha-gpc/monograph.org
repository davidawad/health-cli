** Identity & forms
- L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine (GPC, choline alfoscerate/alphoscerate) is a water-soluble, choline-containing phospholipid metabolite of phosphatidylcholine. It is sold as both a pharmaceutical product and a dietary supplement [PMID:40036805] [PMID:36950691].
- Regulatory status differs by country. It has had marketing authorization since 1985, and countries disagree on whether it should be a prescription medicine [PMID:36683513]. Reviews describe it as available as a prescription drug [PMID:40842650].
  - Italy: Gliatilin (Italfarmaco; ATC N07AX02), 600 mg soft capsules, is an authorized medicine "soggetto a prescrizione medica" (prescription only), reimbursement class C (paid by the patient), authorized 1993 [URL:https://api.aifa.gov.it/aifa-bdf-eif-be/1.0.0/formadosaggio/ricerca?query=gliatilin].
  - South Korea: a prescription drug covered by National Health Insurance. It is widely prescribed for cognitive impairment and costs the insurer a lot [PMID:39548376] [PMID:42361543]. A tablet formulation was shown bioequivalent to the Gliatilin soft capsule in Korean volunteers [PMID:31040642]. Korean manufacturers registered placebo-controlled phase IV re-evaluation trials in AD, degenerative MCI and vascular MCI [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05383183] [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05041790] [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05050604].
  - Russia/CIS: registered as a drug (e.g. Cereton, Gliatilin) in injectable, capsule and oral-solution forms. It is recommended in Russian AD guidelines but not in US, EU or UK guidelines, nor on the WHO Model EML [PMID:41467376] [PMID:42133414].
  - Food status: approved as a new food resource in Canada (2023) and China (2024) [PMID:39898924]. EFSA (2026) concluded that soy-lecithin-derived L-alpha-GPC is safe as a novel food at up to 203.7 mg/day (82.5 mg/day choline) for people over 3 years of age [PMID:42131868].
- Choline content: 203.7 mg GPC supplies about 82.5 mg choline (about 40% by weight) [PMID:42131868].
- Typical forms: 400 mg and 600 mg soft capsules, tablets, oral solution, and IM/IV injection (1000 mg) in Russian and Italian clinical use [PMID:31040642] [PMID:38696157] [PMID:1428296].

** Mechanisms
- Choline donor. In the gut mucosa GPC is cleaved to glycerophosphate and free choline [PMID:1662399]. Intestinal glycerophosphodiesterase Gpcpd1 hydrolyses it in the lumen [PMID:39510189]. Oral GPC raises plasma choline within 60-120 min [PMID:22673596]. In one study plasma choline AUC was similar to choline chloride, bitartrate and egg-PC at equal choline doses [PMID:34287673].
- IM alpha-GPC 1000 mg raised plasma choline faster and higher than an equal dose of citicoline [PMID:1428296].
- Cholinergic effects in rats: raises acetylcholine release in the hippocampus and partly prevents the scopolamine-induced fall in brain ACh. It reverses scopolamine amnesia [PMID:1662399] [PMID:1409797]. It increases expression of the vesicular ACh transporter and choline transporter [PMID:21195433].
- Other proposed actions, mostly preclinical: phosphatidylinositol signal transduction [PMID:1409797], neurotrophic and anti-inflammatory signalling via alpha7 nAChR in microglia [PMID:38391922], hippocampal neurogenesis, and neurovascular-unit protection [PMID:36212684] [PMID:40036805] [PMID:42199923].
- Growth hormone: cholinergic tone enhances GH release. Alpha-GPC increased the GH response to GHRH in young and elderly volunteers [PMID:1577400].
- Methyl donor and TMAO pathway: choline from GPC is oxidised to betaine. Gut microbes also convert it to trimethylamine, which the liver oxidises to TMAO [PMID:34287673] [PMID:21475195]. TMAO formation after GPC is partly Gpcpd1-dependent in mice [PMID:39510189]. In ApoE-/- mice GPC promoted atherosclerosis and enriched cutC-encoding bacteria [PMID:34948275].

** Dosing in trials
- Dementia (Italian/Latin American trials, ASCOMALVA): 400 mg three times daily (1200 mg/d) for 180 days to 4 years [PMID:12637119] [PMID:28035924]. ASCOMALVA added it to donepezil 10 mg/d [PMID:35719710].
- MCI: 600 mg/d for 12 weeks [PMID:39300341]. 1200 mg/d for 3-12 months [PMID:29927403] [PMID:39703111]. Russian regimens used 20 IV infusions of 1000 mg yearly [PMID:38696157] or repeated 3-month oral courses of 400 mg three times daily [PMID:42054337].
- Vascular cognitive impairment: 600 mg twice daily with nimodipine for 1 year [PMID:33855653].
- Healthy-adult and exercise studies: 315-630 mg single dose [PMID:39683633], 600 mg/d for 6 days [PMID:26582972], 1000 mg single dose [PMID:22673596] [PMID:36235623], and 300 mg/d inside a multi-ingredient product [PMID:37229544].
- Food-supplement ceiling assessed by EFSA: 203.7 mg/day [PMID:42131868].
- PK: after 1200 mg oral, plasma choline Cmax was about 0.37 ug/mL with Tmax about 3.5-3.9 h (baseline-corrected) [PMID:31040642].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Trials: no excess AEs versus placebo and no serious AEs in a 12-week MCI RCT [PMID:39300341]. Tolerability was good in the 180-day AD RCT [PMID:12637119]. In CONIVaD, 4 of 62 dropped out for adverse reactions and 8 had non-serious reactions (combination with nimodipine) [PMID:33855653]. Reviews report no severe adverse effects in human studies [PMID:40036805] [PMID:24156263]. Side effects (brief) occurred in 15% with IV use in Parkinson's disease [PMID:20032953].
- Stroke signal: in 12 million Koreans aged >=50, prescribed users had higher 10-year stroke risk (matched aHR total 1.43, ischemic 1.34, hemorrhagic 1.37) with dose-response [PMID:34817582]. This is confounded by indication: users were older and prescribed it for cognitive or vascular complaints. Note: the brief cited "Kim et al. 2023"; the study PubMed returns is Lee G et al., JAMA Netw Open 2021 [PMID:34817582].
  - A later Korean MCI cohort found lower stroke risk in users who did not progress to dementia [PMID:40155153].
- TMAO and atherosclerosis: water-soluble choline supplements, including GPC, rapidly raised plasma TMAO in a crossover study, unlike egg-PC [PMID:34287673] [PMID:36840817]. In a 12-man RCT, GPC at 480 mg-choline-equivalent did not significantly raise TMAO [PMID:38490741]. GPC promoted atherosclerosis in mice [PMID:34948275].
  - Higher plasma TMAO is associated with MACCE (HR 1.67) and mortality (HR 1.91) [PMID:29020409] and with acute ischemic stroke (RR 1.12 per 1 umol/L) [PMID:37883346]. Reviews call the risk unvalidated and debatable [PMID:40036805] [PMID:42196986].
- Cancer: no association with kidney cancer in a Korean cohort (aHR 0.95, 0.84-1.08) [PMID:42361543].
- Toxicology: rat oral LD50 >=10 g/kg and not genotoxic. 26-week NOAEL 150 mg/kg/day [PMID:21414376]. A newer package found a 90-day NOAEL of 1000 mg/kg (female rats) and maternal NOAEL of 1000 mg/kg in a teratogenicity study [PMID:39577616] [PMID:42131868].
- Cardiomyocytes in vitro: 24-h exposure was cytotoxic, while short exposure was cytoprotective. The authors call for cardiac safety testing [PMID:31280435].
- Seizure models: acetylcholine precursors had timing-dependent effects (early CDP-choline worsened post-seizure neuronal death; late alpha-GPC was protective) [PMID:28521701].

** Contraindications
- No formal contraindication is established from trials [PMID:39300341] [PMID:40036805].
- Pregnancy and lactation: EFSA's novel-food assessment included pregnant and lactating women at <=203.7 mg/day. Data on pharmacological doses in pregnancy are lacking [PMID:42131868].
- Prior stroke or high cerebrovascular/atherosclerotic risk: caution because of the observational stroke signal and the TMAO mechanism [PMID:34817582] [PMID:34948275].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Donepezil and other cholinesterase inhibitors: synergy, the best-studied combination. Preclinically the combination raised brain ACh more than either alone. In ASCOMALVA, donepezil + 1200 mg/d alpha-GPC slowed decline vs donepezil + placebo [PMID:24898643] [PMID:22959283]. A Korean RCT showed additional ADAS-Cog benefit [PMID:38875437]. A Korean CDM cohort found no benefit in AChEI users [PMID:39512703]. No excess cholinergic AEs were reported, but systematic safety data for the combination are limited.
- Pyridostigmine: one month of GPC did not restore the pyridostigmine potentiation of GHRH-induced GH in anorexia nervosa [PMID:12952362].
- Anticholinergic drugs (including scopolamine): pharmacodynamic opposition. Alpha-GPC reversed scopolamine amnesia and partly restored brain ACh in rats [PMID:1662399] [PMID:1409797]. Anticholinergic burden is associated with dementia risk [PMID:33933505]. No human interaction study exists.
- Nimodipine: co-administered for 1 year in CONIVaD without safety concerns, but with no efficacy gain [PMID:33855653].
- Antidepressants: adjunct to antidepressants in an older-adult MDD trial (completed; results pending) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05257902].
*** Other supplements
- Citicoline: an overlapping choline donor. Alpha-GPC gave higher plasma choline than citicoline [PMID:1428296]. It differed in cholinergic transporter effects in rats [PMID:21195433]. It was superior on SCAG in dementia in an MA [PMID:41426989]. Stacking duplicates choline/TMAO load (mechanistic inference) [PMID:34287673].
- Trimethylglycine (TMG): GPC-derived choline is oxidised to betaine [PMID:34287673]. Betaine supplements produced a borderline TMAO rise (p=0.053) [PMID:38876040]. Both feed the same pathway [PMID:21475195]. This is mechanistic inference.
- Acetyl-L-Carnitine: compared head-to-head as donepezil add-ons. The donepezil + ALCAR arm performed worse than donepezil + alpha-GPC [PMID:38875437]. Carnitine is also a gut-microbial TMAO precursor, giving additive TMAO load (mechanistic inference) [PMID:30530985].
- Huperzine A (not in corpus; a natural AChE inhibitor): expected synergy similar to the ChE-I + alpha-GPC rationale (mechanistic inference) [PMID:24086396] [PMID:24898643].
- Caffeine: no combination study of alpha-GPC with caffeine was found. One multi-ingredient study (BCAA + citrulline + 300 mg A-GPC) improved cycling peak power [PMID:37229544]. Another (A-GPC + uridine + DHA) did not improve power or cognition [PMID:28222577].
*** Conditions
- Cerebrovascular disease / stroke risk: observational harm signal [PMID:34817582]; contrasting findings in MCI [PMID:40155153].
- Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease: TMAO pathway concerns (mechanistic plus animal data) [PMID:34948275] [PMID:29020409].
- Anorexia nervosa: no effect of GPC on cholinergic GH control [PMID:12952362].
*** Lab-test interference
- No assay interference was identified. Alpha-GPC raises plasma choline, betaine and TMAO (true physiological changes) [PMID:34287673]. It may raise GH acutely [PMID:22673596] and free fatty acids/ketones [PMID:22673596]. In a T2DM RCT it improved the LDL/HDL ratio and waist circumference (OR 0.16 for a high LDL/HDL ratio) [PMID:40095622].

** Genotype notes
- APOE: in an open trial in amnestic MCI (1200 mg/d, 3 months), APOE4 non-carriers performed better on immediate and delayed 10-word recall than carriers [PMID:29927403]. This is small and uncontrolled.
- MTHFR C677T modified betaine levels after betaine supplementation (relevant to the choline-betaine pathway, not tested with GPC) [PMID:38876040].

** Latest research
- [2026-08] Korean NHIS cohort (81,970 users vs 409,801 non-users): no association with kidney cancer (aHR 0.95) [PMID:42361543].
- [2026-05] EFSA NDA Panel: L-alpha-GPC from soy lecithin is safe as a novel food at <=203.7 mg/day; MoE 345 for adults [PMID:42131868].
- [2026-05] CARTESIO open-label pilot (n=17, 1200 mg/d, 8 wk): HAMD-17 and GDS-15 improved; no cognitive change [PMID:42278899].
- [2026-05] Narrative review of GPC absorption, gut microbiota and TMAO: the adverse role of TMAO remains debatable [PMID:42196986].
- [2026-05] CIS regulatory analysis: choline alfoscerate is registered across CIS and recommended in Russian AD guidelines only [PMID:41467376].
- [2026] Neurodegeneration review: evidence heterogeneous; calls for biomarker-based RCTs [PMID:42199923].
- [2026] Russian 5-year comparative study in first-degree relatives of AD patients (n=122): 16.7% SCI-to-MCI conversion in untreated controls; improved scores with repeated oral courses (non-randomized) [PMID:42054337].
- [2026-01] Preterm infants: GPC raised plasma choline fastest, with similar AUC to other choline forms [PMID:41524941].
- [2025] Alpha-GPC vs citicoline MA in dementia (3 RCTs): SCAG WMD -3.92 favoring alpha-GPC [PMID:41426989].
- [2025-10] RCT of 315 mg/d alpha-GPC for 6 weeks on cognition and sports performance started (industry sponsor) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07267845].
- [2025] Russian MA of Gliatilin in non-dementia vascular/post-traumatic MCI (10 studies): significant effect, high heterogeneity [PMID:41362980].
- [2025-08] Nutr Rev mechanism/safety review: no severe AEs; stroke/atherosclerosis risk awaits validation [PMID:40036805].
- [2025-04] Korean MCI cohort (n=508,107): lower progression to AD (HR 0.899) and VaD (HR 0.832) [PMID:40155153].
- [2025-03] T2DM-MCI RCT (n=36, 1200 mg/d): MMSE MD +1.7 at 12 months [PMID:39703111].
- [2025-03] Canada (2023) and China (2024) approved it as a new food resource [PMID:39898924].
- [2025-02] T2DM depressive symptoms RCT (n=49, 800 mg/d): not superior to placebo [PMID:40095622].
- [2024-12] Acute crossover RCT in resistance-trained men: Stroop improved; no GH or performance effect [PMID:39683633].
- [2024-11] NHIS senior dementia cohort: about 20% lower mortality; unclear effect on progression [PMID:39548376].
- [2024-10] Korean multicenter CDM cohort: no association with MCI-to-dementia progression (HR 0.93) [PMID:39512703].

** Open questions
- Whether the Korean stroke signal is causal or reflects confounding by indication, and whether TMAO mediates it in humans [PMID:34817582] [PMID:42196986].
- Results of the Korean placebo-controlled phase IV re-evaluation trials in AD and MCI (status unknown as of the registry check) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05383183] [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05041790].
- Final (4-year) ASCOMALVA results. Most published data are interim analyses from one group [PMID:32508323].
- Independent replication of MCI benefit using modern diagnostic and biomarker criteria [PMID:38929542] [PMID:42199923].
- Ergogenic and GH effects in larger, longer trials; evidence is limited to small crossover studies [PMID:26582972] [PMID:22673596].
- Long-term safety at supplement doses in healthy adults, including cardiovascular outcomes.
