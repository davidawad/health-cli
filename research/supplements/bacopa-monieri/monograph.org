** Identity & forms
- Bacopa monnieri (often written Bacopa monnieri, common name Brahmi) is an aquatic herb. Trials use extracts standardized to bacosides, but extraction and standardization differ. [PMID:38671841]

** Mechanisms
- Bacosides have antioxidant, cholinergic and synaptic signaling effects in preclinical models; human cognitive mechanism is not established. [PMID:38671841]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Memory / cognition in healthy adults | Small RCTs and meta-analyses | possible delayed recall benefit | Older trials suggest modest memory effects; outcome heterogeneity and extract differences limit estimates | low | PMID:38671841 |
| Mild cognitive impairment | Triple-blind placebo RCT | mixed | 62 participants, 160 mg extract for 2 months: no clear overall cognitive or sleep benefit; some subdomains changed | low | PMID:38538390 |
| Working memory | 2026 network meta-analysis | possible benefit at higher doses | High-dose category ranked favorably, but indirect comparisons and unusually large effects require replication | very low | PMID:41678913 |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials typically used standardized extracts around  של 300–600 mg/day for 8–12 weeks; some MCI study used 160 mg/day. Extract bacoside standardization varies, so mass is not directly comparable. [PMID:38538390, PMID:41678913]

** Safety & adverse effects
- GI effects (nausea, cramping, diarrhea), fatigue and increased bowel movements are common; may cause bradycardia or thyroid effects based on limited evidence. [PMID:38671841]

** Contraindications
- Caution with bradycardia, thyroid disease, GI disorders, pregnancy/lactation and cholinergic therapy; clinical interaction data are sparse. [PMID:38671841]

** Interactions
- Potential additive cholinergic effects with cholinesterase inhibitors and anticholinergic medicines; possible thyroid hormone effects and CYP enzyme modulation are mostly mechanistic/preclinical. No established assay interference. [PMID:38671841]

** Latest research (last 24 months)
- 2024 RCT in mild cognitive impairment found no consistent total cognition or sleep benefit after 2 months, with selected attention/verbal fluency signals. 2026 network meta-analysis suggests cognitive effects but relies on indirect, heterogeneous comparisons. [PMID:38538390, PMID:41678913]

** Open questions
- Replicate effects with preregistered trials using chemically characterized extracts, clinically meaningful outcomes and adequate follow-up.
