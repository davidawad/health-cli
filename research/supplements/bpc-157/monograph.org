** Identity & forms
- Synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide described as a gastric-derived sequence; marketed in unapproved products. Human formulation, purity and route standardization are unresolved. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Mechanisms
- Preclinical reports propose angiogenic, nitric-oxide and cytoprotective signaling effects; these are predominantly animal/cell findings and do not establish human benefit. [PMID:38613439]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Musculoskeletal healing | Preclinical review; sparse uncontrolled human reports | unknown | No reliable controlled human effect estimate | very low | PMID:38613439 |
| Inflammatory bowel disease | Small exploratory human study reports | uncertain | Insufficient controlled published evidence | very low | URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download |
| Safety | Small human exposures and FDA review | unknown | Safety monitoring limited; short animal studies signal possible coagulation/liver effects | very low | URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download |

** Dosing in trials
- Exploratory human research reported rectal enema, intra-articular, intravesical and IV routes; FDA briefing lists heterogeneous doses and very small cohorts. No established safe/effective dose. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Safety & adverse effects
- FDA review notes limited toxicology; repeat-dose animal studies raised coagulation and liver signals, longer duration studies unavailable. Adverse event reports exist but attribution uncertain. Product quality and immunogenicity risks are inadequately characterized. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Contraindications
- No approved indication or established safety. Avoid treating research chemical use as equivalent to licensed medicine; special uncertainty applies to pregnancy, lactation, liver/coagulation disorders and injectable use. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Interactions
- Potential coagulation and liver effects create theoretical risk with anticoagulants, antiplatelets and hepatotoxic drugs; direct interaction studies are absent. No validated lab-test interference established. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Latest research (last 24 months)
- 2026 FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee briefing described small heterogeneous human exposures and limited safety data; identified potential coagulation/liver signals in 28-day animal studies. [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/193773/download]

** Open questions
- Determine molecular identity, formulation quality, pharmacokinetics, immunogenicity, dose-ranging, controlled efficacy, and long-term toxicity before therapeutic conclusions.
