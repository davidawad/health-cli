** Identity & forms
- Citicoline (CDP-choline, cytidine 5'-diphosphocholine) is the endogenous intermediate of the Kennedy (CDP-choline) pathway of phosphatidylcholine synthesis [PMID:11796739] [PMID:24504829].
- Regulatory status differs by country. In the US and EU it is sold as a supplement / novel food ingredient. In many other countries it is a prescription or registered medicine (oral, IV, IM): it is "approved in some countries" for acute ischemic stroke [PMID:22691567] and was approved for TBI in 59 countries when COBRIT was run [PMID:23168823]. It is on national essential-medicine lists in several CIS countries [PMID:41467376]. RX_STATUS here reflects the US/EU supplement market; in those other countries it is an Rx drug.
- EU: authorised as a novel food ingredient (Decision 2014/423/EU). Limits are 500 mg/day in food supplements and 250 mg/serving (max 1000 mg/day) in foods for special medical purposes. Products are aimed at adults and not intended for children [URL:https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2014/423/introduction].
- Cognizin is a branded citicoline from Kyowa Hakko. It was used in the healthy-adult memory RCT (500 mg/d) [PMID:33978188], the adolescent attention RCT (250-500 mg/d) [PMID:26179181] and 31P-MRS studies [PMID:18816480].
- Ophthalmic forms include an oral solution [PMID:36639525] and 2% eye drops, alone or with vitamin B12 [PMID:31725734] [PMID:32180131].

** Mechanisms
- After oral intake citicoline is hydrolysed in the gut wall and liver. A 14C study (300 mg) found near-complete absorption (<1% in faeces), plasma radioactivity peaks at 1 h and 24 h, and elimination mainly as CO2 [PMID:6412727]. In humans, oral doses raise plasma choline in a dose-related way and plasma uridine by 70-120%, and cytidine is undetectable. This suggests uridine plus choline are the circulating precursors [PMID:10974208].
- Proposed effects: membrane phosphatidylcholine restoration, reduced phospholipase A2 activation and lipid peroxidation, restored Na+/K+-ATPase, extra choline for acetylcholine, and raised dopamine release / tyrosine hydroxylase activity [PMID:11796739] [PMID:39221499]. A critical review says no adequate mechanistic description yet exists [PMID:24504829].
- Brain bioenergetics: 6 weeks of 500 or 2000 mg/d Cognizin raised frontal phosphocreatine (+7%) and beta-NTP (+14%) on 31P-MRS in 16 healthy adults (uncontrolled) [PMID:18816480].
- Preclinical stroke MA (14 studies, 522 animals): infarct volume fell 27.8% and neurological deficit improved 20.2% [PMID:22845688].

** Dosing in trials
- Acute stroke: 1000 mg q12h IV for 3 days, then oral, for 6 weeks (ICTUS) [PMID:22691567] [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00331890]. Oral 500-2000 mg/d in earlier trials [PMID:12468781] [PMID:32860632].
- TBI: 1000 mg twice daily enteral/oral for 90 days (COBRIT) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00545662]. 1000 mg/d for 90 days in a 2026 pilot RCT [PMID:42679920].
- Cognition: 1000 mg/d for 12 months after stroke [PMID:23406981]; 1000 mg/d added to AChEI [PMID:28035929]; 500 mg/d (Cognizin) in healthy older adults [PMID:33978188]; 1000-2000 mg/d in the Spiers trial [PMID:8624220].
- Glaucoma: 2% eye drops three times daily for 3 years [PMID:32541370]; oral solution in cycles [PMID:36639525].
- Psychiatry / addiction: titrated to 2000 mg/d [PMID:30457668] [PMID:22974472]; 200 mg/d adjunct to citalopram [PMID:28085707].
- Parkinson's: 1000 mg/d IM, two 6-week cycles over 24 weeks [PMID:42561742]; 1200 mg/d oral in older trials [PMID:2289218].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Large RCTs found no safety signal: ICTUS showed no differences in safety variables [PMID:22691567], and COBRIT showed none in 1213 TBI patients [PMID:23168823]. The Cochrane review found little or no difference in serious cardiovascular AEs (RR 1.04, 0.84-1.29) but rated harms data as low quality [PMID:32860632].
- AEs reported with AChEI combinations were self-limiting excitability, gastric intolerance and headache [PMID:32538854]. In an amphetamine NMA citicoline was among the most tolerable options (dropout RR 0.69) [PMID:41394525].
- No mood worsening in bipolar trials [PMID:25998279] [PMID:17873684].
- One pediatric case report describes recurrent suicidality on rechallenge with citicoline plus phosphatidylserine during methylphenidate treatment [PMID:41562580].
- EFSA judged citicoline safe at proposed novel-food uses (up to 1000 mg/day across foods) [URL:https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2014/423/introduction].
- TMAO: water-soluble choline salts and alpha-GPC raise TMAO quickly in a crossover study, whereas egg phosphatidylcholine does not [PMID:34287673]. A 4-week trial of ~400 mg/d choline did not change TMAO [PMID:29764315]. No citicoline-specific TMAO study was found. Choline metabolites may be a "two-edged sword" in stroke risk [PMID:40054542] [PMID:38128611].

** Contraindications
- The EU novel food authorisation says it is not intended for children [URL:https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudn/2014/423/introduction]. Pediatric trials (ADHD, autism, amblyopia) are small [PMID:38976279] [PMID:40576847] [PMID:24618483].
- No pregnancy or lactation trial data were found; a search returned no relevant studies.
- Cholinergic/dopaminergic activity suggests caution when combined with stimulants in children (single case report) [PMID:41562580].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Levodopa: citicoline allowed levodopa dose cuts of up to 50% in older trials [PMID:33279231] [PMID:2289218]. It gave added motor benefit in a 2026 RCT on stable PD medication [PMID:42561742]. Watch for additive dopaminergic effects.
- Cholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) and memantine: combination studies are mostly retrospective and suggest small additive MMSE benefits with no new safety signals [PMID:28035929] [PMID:27587069] [PMID:33484469] [PMID:33459645] [PMID:32538854].
- Anticholinergic drugs: no direct study found. MCI reviews advise reducing anticholinergic load [PMID:41562827], which would also be expected to oppose a cholinergic precursor (inference).
- SSRIs (citalopram): adjunct improved depression scores [PMID:28085707].
- Antipsychotics (risperidone): adjunct improved negative symptoms with no extra extrapyramidal effects [PMID:29901250].
- Stimulants (methylphenidate): case report of suicidality with citicoline plus phosphatidylserine [PMID:41562580].
- Amantadine: added nothing to amantadine alone in a TBI RCT [PMID:41027417].
- rtPA / thrombolysis: ICTUS tracked symptomatic ICH after rtPA and found no safety differences [PMID:22691567]. Benefit in the sponsor MA was mainly in non-rtPA patients [PMID:27234918].
*** Other supplements
- Alpha-GPC (choline alfoscerate): duplicative choline source. Alpha-GPC raised plasma choline more than IM citicoline [PMID:1428296] and beat citicoline on SCAG in a dementia MA [PMID:41426989]. Both are reviewed together as choline supplements [PMID:36950691].
- Caffeine: a citicoline-caffeine beverage improved attention and reaction time in healthy adults [PMID:25046515].
- Cerebrolysin: combined use in severe TBI showed a nonsignificant trend [PMID:41409218] [PMID:38748062].
- Acetyl-L-Carnitine and N-Acetyl-L-Cysteine (NAC): proposed synergy on complementary pathways (review only) [PMID:42136265].
- EPA/DHA/DPA: DHA plus citicoline improved visual-field indices in glaucoma [PMID:36294916].
- CoQ10: combined for glaucoma neuroprotection (review, mouse OHT) [PMID:36590976] [PMID:40430513].
- Vitamin B12: in combined eye drops for type 1 diabetes retinopathy (pilot) [PMID:32180131].
- Bacopa Monieri and L-Theanine: grouped with citicoline in a narrative "brain health triad" stacking review. No clinical interaction data exist [PMID:42074246].
- Lithium: citicoline and lithium each protected retinal ganglion cells after optic-nerve crush in rats, and the combination was no better than lithium alone [PMID:16876158]. No human data.
*** Conditions
- Bipolar disorder: well tolerated without mood destabilisation in RCTs [PMID:25998279] [PMID:17873684].
- Parkinson's disease: additive to dopaminergic therapy (see Drugs).
- Glaucoma: adjunct to IOP-lowering therapy only. It does not lower IOP [PMID:37768938] [PMID:32541370].
*** Lab-test interference
- No assay interference was found. Citicoline causes true rises in plasma choline and uridine [PMID:10974208] and may raise TMAO (as other water-soluble choline sources do) [PMID:34287673].

** Genotype notes
- APOE: in an open study of 82 first-degree relatives of AD patients, citicoline 1000 mg/d for 3 months gave greater cognitive benefit in APOE4 non-carriers than in carriers [PMID:34870911]. In a cerebrovascular cohort, 24.6% were APOE4 carriers, but no carrier-specific effect was reported [PMID:37489128].
- No cited pharmacogenomic source on CHKA/PCYT1A variants and citicoline response was found.

** Latest research
- [2026-09] Pilot triple-blind RCT (n=60) in mild-to-moderate TBI: 1000 mg/d for 90 days gave higher MMSE (25.2 vs 21.5), Barthel and GOS-E scores [PMID:42679920].
- [2026-08] SR/MA of vascular dementia RCTs: only one citicoline trial qualified, and most trials predate 2013 [PMID:42635820].
- [2026-07] Multicentre DB RCT of add-on citicoline in PD (n=474): MCID OR 2.06, better UPDRS-III, AEs 22.1% vs 27.1% [PMID:42561742].
- [2026-05] Review proposes NAC + ALCAR + citicoline synergy [PMID:42136265].
- [2026-04] Narrative review places citicoline in a Bacopa/L-theanine-based nootropic framework [PMID:42074246].
- [2026-03] SR of citicoline in ophthalmology: possible benefit in amblyopia, glaucoma and NAION, but no consensus on dose [PMID:42179665].
- [2025-12] 12-month placebo-controlled study of oral solution in OAG: PERG/VEP amplitudes improved [PMID:41517474].
- [2025-12] Dementia MA: alpha-GPC outperformed citicoline on SCAG (WMD -3.92) [PMID:41426989].
- [2025-12] Retrospective TBI cohort: citicoline + Cerebrolysin showed a nonsignificant trend [PMID:41409218].
- [2025-11] Case report: suicidality with citicoline + phosphatidylserine on methylphenidate [PMID:41562580].
- [2025-10] Citicoline-amantadine TBI RCT: no gain over amantadine alone [PMID:41027417].
- [2025-06] Open-label RCT in autism: no benefit [PMID:40576847].
- [2025-04] Dose NMA in ischemic stroke (13 studies) found the lowest mortality with 2000 mg/d (mixed-quality studies) [PMID:40255575].
- [2025-03] Review of choline metabolism in stroke: choline and its metabolites (TMAO) can both protect and harm [PMID:40054542].
- [2024-10] SR of cholinergic agents for psychostimulant use disorder: citicoline among few with a positive cocaine trial [PMID:39432105].
- Ongoing: 2% eyedrop glaucoma RCT (n=1000) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05710198]; CITADEL post-delirium [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07804498]; youth alcohol use disorder [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05870111].

** Open questions
- Does any stroke subgroup (non-rtPA, late presentation) truly benefit, given the null ICTUS and Cochrane results [PMID:22691567] [PMID:32860632] versus sponsor MAs [PMID:27234918]?
- Will the large 2% eyedrop RCT confirm slower glaucoma progression [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05710198] [PMID:32541370]?
- Are healthy-adult memory benefits replicable outside sponsor-funded trials? EFSA rejected the claim [PMID:33978188] [PMID:38966137].
- Does long-term citicoline raise TMAO, and does it matter clinically [PMID:34287673] [PMID:40054542]?
- How should the evidence be read given heavy manufacturer involvement (Ferrer, Kyowa) [PMID:16490951] [PMID:27234918] [PMID:33978188]?
