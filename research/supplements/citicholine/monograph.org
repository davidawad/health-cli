** Identity & forms
- Citicoline (CDP-choline) is an intermediate in phosphatidylcholine synthesis and is marketed as a nutritional supplement; in some countries it has been used as a medicine. [PMID:27234918]

** Mechanisms
- Oral citicoline is metabolized to choline and cytidine/uridine, precursors for phospholipid and acetylcholine synthesis; mechanistic plausibility does not prove clinical efficacy. [PMID:36678257]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acute ischemic stroke | Meta-analysis of placebo-controlled RCTs | no clear benefit | Large ICTUS trial and pooled evidence did not demonstrate meaningful recovery benefit | moderate | PMID:27234918 |
| Dementia / cognitive impairment | Systematic review/meta-analysis | possible benefit; uncertain | Seven small heterogeneous studies; effect estimates limited | low | PMID:36678257 |
| Healthy cognitive enhancement | Small RCTs | uncertain | No robust, consistent evidence for durable clinically meaningful benefit | very low | PMID:36678257 |

** Dosing in trials
- Studies commonly used 500–2,000 mg/day orally or parenterally; acute stroke trials used IV/oral schedules over weeks. Indication and route vary. [PMID:27234918, PMID:36678257]

** Safety & adverse effects
- Generally tolerated in trials; possible headache, GI upset, insomnia/restlessness. Long-term safety data for high doses remain limited. [PMID:36678257]

** Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity is a contraindication; caution with bipolar activation/insomnia and pregnancy due to inadequate trial evidence. [PMID:36678257]

** Interactions
- May potentiate levodopa effects through increased dopamine synthesis/release; monitor if combined. No established lab-test interference. [PMID:36678257]

** Latest research (last 24 months)
- 2024 network meta-analysis of acute ischemic stroke neuroprotectants included citicoline but pooled mixed designs and does not overcome prior large null placebo-controlled RCT evidence. [PMID:39575393, PMID:27234918]

** Open questions
- Clarify whether any defined cognitive impairment phenotype benefits and whether outcomes persist beyond treatment.
