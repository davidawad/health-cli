** Identity & forms
- Collagen is a structural extracellular-matrix protein; oral products include hydrolyzed collagen peptides, gelatin, and undenatured type II collagen. These preparations differ and trial evidence is not interchangeable [PMID:38218227].

** Mechanisms
- Hydrolyzed peptides are digested to amino acids and small hydroxyproline-containing peptides; proposed signaling effects on fibroblasts/cartilage remain less established than the clinical endpoint trials. Intact undenatured type II collagen uses a distinct oral-tolerance hypothesis and should not be equated with peptides [PMID:38218227; PMID:42342959].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---------+--------------------+-----------+-------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| Osteoarthritis pain/function | Trial-sequential meta-analysis, 35 RCTs | Small benefit | pain SMD −0.35; function SMD −0.31 | moderate | [PMID:38218227] |
| Skin hydration/elasticity/wrinkles | Systematic review, 25 RCTs | Favorable in many small trials; bias concern | hydration improved in 10/15, elasticity 10/13, wrinkle measures 9/10; high risk of bias common | low | [PMID:42342959] |
| Musculoskeletal/skin outcomes | 2026 umbrella review, 113 RCTs | Favorable associations; mixed outcomes | RCTs across indications; pooled effect varied by outcome | low | [PMID:41809116] |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials use approximately 2.5–15 g/day hydrolyzed peptides for skin, 5–10 g/day peptides for joint symptoms, and 40 mg/day undenatured type II collagen in some OA trials; duration commonly 8–24 weeks [PMID:38218227; PMID:42342959].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Generally well tolerated in RCT syntheses, with no significant increase in adverse events or withdrawals for OA derivatives [PMID:38218227]. Source proteins may include bovine, porcine, marine fish or poultry; allergy/cross-contamination risk depends on source [PMID:42342959].

** Contraindications
- Avoid with known allergy to the source animal/fish; product source and contaminants vary. Pregnancy/lactation and long-term safety are insufficiently studied [PMID:42342959].

** Interactions
- No established clinically important drug interactions or assay interference identified in the cited RCT syntheses; product-specific excipients and allergen source remain relevant [PMID:38218227; PMID:42342959].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source specific to Collagen was identified in this search; no genotype claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2026 umbrella review searched evidence through March 2025 and included 113 RCTs; it reported generally favorable skin, muscle, bone and OA associations, while oral health and cardiometabolic outcomes were mixed [PMID:41809116]. 2025 knee OA meta-analysis updated RCT evidence [PMID:39212129].

** Open questions
- Larger, better reported human studies with standardized products, clinically meaningful endpoints and longer follow-up are needed; current gaps are noted above.
