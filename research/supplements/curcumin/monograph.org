** Identity & forms
- Curcumin is represented by these commonly studied or marketed forms: turmeric, Curcuma longa, curcuminoids. Clinical products and formulations can differ in composition and bioavailability; this limits cross-trial comparisons [PMID:39478418, PMID:40538540, PMID:39425780].

** Mechanisms
- Proposed mechanisms are outcome- and formulation-dependent; mechanistic plausibility does not establish clinical efficacy. Human evidence is summarized below [PMID:39478418, PMID:40538540, PMID:39425780].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Osteoarthritis symptoms and inflammation: possible benefit, generally low/very low certainty | Systematic review/meta-analysis or human trial synthesis | Mixed/possible benefit; no established clinical disease prevention | See evidence summary below; pooled estimates vary by outcome and population | low | PMID:39478418, PMID:40538540, PMID:39425780 |

- 103 RCT meta-analysis (7,216 participants) found statistically significant pooled results for 23/42 outcomes; certainty high for fasting glucose, CRP, HDL and weight, with most other outcomes low/very low [PMID:39478418]. An umbrella review found underlying review quality generally poor [PMID:40538540].
- Osteoarthritis symptoms and inflammation: possible benefit, generally low/very low certainty; metabolic markers: mixed but signals for glucose and CRP; no demonstrated disease-modifying or mortality benefit [PMID:39478418, PMID:40538540].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials used heterogeneous turmeric extracts, curcuminoids, micellar/nano and piperine-enhanced products, typically hundreds of mg to several grams daily for weeks to months; products are not dose-equivalent [PMID:39478418].

** Safety & adverse effects
- GI upset, headache and rash are reported; rare clinically apparent liver injury has been reported with turmeric/curcumin products, especially enhanced-bioavailability formulations [PMID:39425780, URL:https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548561/].

** Contraindications
- Avoid treating as interchangeable with dietary turmeric; caution with biliary obstruction/gallstones and during cancer therapy because clinical interaction evidence is incomplete [PMID:39425780].

** Interactions
- Potential antiplatelet effects and drug-metabolism interactions are largely mechanistic; clinical interaction evidence with anticoagulants is sparse and heterogeneous [PMID:39288907]. Piperine-containing formulations can alter drug exposure [PMID:39478418].
- No genotype-specific clinical claim is made because no qualifying pharmacogenomic source was identified for this research set.

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source identified for a clinically useful genotype-specific note.

** Latest research
- 2024–2026: 2025 umbrella review: 25 prior reviews; methodological quality relatively poor [PMID:40538540]. 2025 cancer-care review reports small heterogeneous adjunct trials and insufficient basis for routine use [PMID:39425780].

** Open questions
- Larger, preregistered, adequately powered human trials with standardized, chemically characterized products and longer follow-up are needed to establish clinically important benefits and characterize uncommon harms.
