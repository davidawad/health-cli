** Identity & forms
- Dexedrine is dextroamphetamine sulfate, an oral immediate-release tablet or extended-release Spansule prescription CNS stimulant, Schedule II in the United States [DailyMed].

** Mechanisms
- Amphetamine increases synaptic norepinephrine and dopamine through transporter and vesicular mechanisms; FDA labeling describes sympathomimetic CNS stimulation [DailyMed].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |

| ADHD symptoms | regulatory label based on clinical trials | improvement in indicated populations | label supports pediatric ADHD; exact effect varies by trial | moderate | DailyMed |
| Narcolepsy symptoms | regulatory label | symptom control | approved indication; no pooled effect size in label | moderate | DailyMed |

** Dosing in trials
- FDA label gives individualized titration; pediatric ADHD starts 5 mg once or twice daily, with weekly 5 mg increments, rarely exceeding 40 mg/day; dosing differs by indication and formulation [DailyMed].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Label warns of abuse, misuse, addiction, serious cardiac disease risk, increased blood pressure/heart rate, psychiatric reactions, growth suppression, and peripheral vasculopathy [DailyMed].

** Contraindications
- Contraindicated with hypersensitivity to amphetamine and during MAOI treatment or within 14 days after stopping MAOI because of hypertensive-crisis risk; label also lists advanced arteriosclerosis, symptomatic cardiovascular disease, moderate/severe hypertension, hyperthyroidism, glaucoma, agitated state, and history of drug abuse [DailyMed].

** Interactions
- Drugs, supplements, conditions, and relevant laboratory interference are summarized in the structured interaction object. MAOIs contraindicated; serotonergic drugs and CYP2D6 inhibitors increase serotonin-syndrome/exposure risk; alkalinizing agents may raise levels, acidifying agents may lower levels; antihypertensives may be opposed [DailyMed]. No genotype-specific claim is made because no directly relevant pharmacogenomic source was identified.

** Latest research
- 2025 label version on DailyMed reiterates boxed warnings, contraindications, and interaction precautions; label updates are regulatory rather than new efficacy trials [DailyMed].

** Open questions
- Long-term comparative benefit-risk, individualized dosing, and durable outcomes remain under study; these statements are within FDA label scope [DailyMed].
