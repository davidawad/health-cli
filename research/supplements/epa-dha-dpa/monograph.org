** Identity & forms
- Long-chain omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids include EPA, DHA, and DPA; fish oil and algal oils differ in composition and formulations. Prescription EPA products are distinct from OTC mixed oils. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Omega3FattyAcids-HealthProfessional/]

** Mechanisms
- Proposed biological activity and the best-established physiological context are described above; clinical mechanisms remain uncertain where outcome evidence is limited. ["PMID:41445624"]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | Certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary studied clinical/biomarker outcomes | Systematic review/meta-analysis or RCT synthesis | Meta-analysis of 38 RCTs (149,051 people) found modest reduction in several cardiovascular outcomes, stronger for EPA-only than EPA+DHA; AF increased (RR 1.26) | See narrative | low | PMID:41445624; PMID:39076869 |
| Long-term clinical outcomes | Evidence insufficient or mixed | No established benefit | Not established | very low | PMID:41445624; PMID:39076869 |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials use ~0.5–4 g/day EPA+DHA; prescription icosapent ethyl is 4 g/day. DPA is usually present as a minor constituent, not separately dosed. [PMID:41445624] [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Omega3FattyAcids-HealthProfessional/]

** Safety & adverse effects
- GI effects; high-dose therapy associated with atrial fibrillation; possible bleeding at high purified EPA exposure. [PMID:34505026] [PMID:41445624]

** Contraindications
- Caution with atrial fibrillation history and high-dose regimens. [PMID:41445624]

** Interactions
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets: monitor bleeding at pharmacologic doses; may lower triglycerides with lipid-lowering therapy. [PMID:34505026]

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed; no genotype-specific claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2025–2026 literature: 2025 meta-analysis across 34 trials found dose and baseline CVD risk modified observed AF association. [PMID:41445624]

** Open questions
- Whether findings translate into durable patient-important outcomes, optimal formulation/dose, and long-term safety remains uncertain. [PMID:41445624; PMID:39076869]
