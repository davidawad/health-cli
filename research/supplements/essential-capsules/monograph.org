** Identity & forms
- Branded multi-ingredient dietary supplement ("daily longevity multivitamin") sold by Blueprint (Bryan Johnson). It is a vitamin-mineral premix plus NAD+ precursor, polyphenols, ubiquinol, spermidine, a probiotic, boron and lithium. Serving: 2 veggie capsules once daily in the morning with food; 30 servings per container [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essentials-capsules].
- Label per 2 capsules (official product page, fetched 2026-10-13; identical amounts in NIH DSLD label 328204, entered 2025-02-23) [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essentials-capsules] [URL:https://dsld.od.nih.gov/label/328204]:
  - Vitamins: D3 (cholecalciferol) 50 mcg/2000 IU; E (d-alpha-tocopherol) 67 mg (447% DV); B1 (thiamine HCl) 1.1 mg; B2 1.4 mg; niacin (niacinamide) 15 mg; B6 (pyridoxine HCl) 1.4 mg; folate (calcium L-5-MTHF) 200 mcg DFE; B12 (methylcobalamin) 125 mcg; biotin 50 mcg; pantothenic acid 6 mg.
  - Minerals: calcium 50 mg; iodine (potassium iodide) 200 mcg; zinc (bisglycinate) 15 mg; selenium (L-selenomethionine) 28 mcg; manganese (citrate) 1 mg; boron (glycinate) 3 mg; lithium (orotate) 1 mg elemental.
  - Non-nutrients: nicotinamide riboside chloride 300 mg; broccoli seed extract 200 mg (glucoraphanin 20 mg); fisetin 100 mg (smoketree stem extract); luteolin 100 mg (Sophora japonica bud extract); ubiquinol 50 mg; Lactobacillus acidophilus 20 mg (4 billion CFU); spermidine (trihydrochloride) 10 mg.
  - Other ingredients: HPMC/pectin capsule, rice hulls, starch, rice blend extract.
- Version caveats (from the two label sources above): the page says "23 longevity actives" but the facts panel and DSLD list 24 rows. Its ingredient notes name "Potassium Iodate" while the facts panel says potassium iodide. DSLD lists calcium as carbonate plus dicalcium phosphate (page: dicalcium phosphate only). DSLD records biotin as "50 mg" at 167% DV; 167% of the 30 mcg DV is 50 mcg, as on the page, so the DSLD unit looks like a transcription error. Blueprint revises formulas, so the bottle in hand governs.
- Lithium orotate supplies about 3.83 mg elemental lithium per 100 mg salt [PMID:18072162]. 1 mg elemental lithium is therefore about 26 mg lithium orotate, far below prescription lithium doses [PMID:34146638].
- No randomized or pharmacokinetic study of the combined product was found in PubMed (search 2026-10-13). All efficacy statements below are ingredient-level extrapolations.

** Mechanisms
- Micronutrients (D, E, B vitamins, zinc, selenium, iodine, manganese) act as cofactors, hormone precursors or antioxidant enzyme components. Multivitamin trials test this "fill nutrient gaps" model [PMID:35294969] [PMID:35727272].
- Nicotinamide riboside (NR) is an NAD+ precursor. 100, 300 and 1000 mg/d raised whole-blood NAD+ by 22%, 51% and 142% in 8 weeks [PMID:31278280]. Raising NAD+ has produced few clinically relevant effects in humans [PMID:37478182].
- Glucoraphanin is converted (by myrosinase or gut bacteria) to sulforaphane, an Nrf2 inducer. A broccoli sprout beverage increased benzene detoxication only at its highest dose [PMID:31268126].
- Fisetin and luteolin are flavonoids. Fisetin is proposed as a senolytic/anti-inflammatory agent from in vitro and animal data. Unformulated fisetin has very low oral bioavailability (Cmax about 10 ng/mL after 1000 mg) [PMID:42633989] [PMID:36304817]. Luteolin has poor oral bioavailability, circulates mainly as glucuronide/sulfate conjugates, and inhibits OATP1B1/OATP2B1 in vitro [PMID:38088265].
- Ubiquinol is reduced CoQ10, a mitochondrial electron carrier. Absorbed CoQ10 appears in blood mostly as ubiquinol whichever form is taken. Ubiquinol was not significantly more bioavailable than ubiquinone (1.7-fold, CI 0.9-3.1) [PMID:32188111].
- Spermidine is an autophagy-inducing polyamine in models. Oral spermidine 15 mg/d raised plasma spermine, not spermidine, suggesting presystemic conversion [PMID:37111071].
- Lithium inhibits GSK3-beta. In mice, dietary lithium depletion accelerated amyloid/tau pathology and lithium orotate replacement prevented it; human brain lithium was lower in MCI [PMID:40770094]. Whether orotate differs from carbonate after ingestion is disputed: it is predicted to dissociate to Li+ in the stomach [PMID:42312809].

** Dosing in trials
- Multivitamin trials used conventional RDA-level products (Centrum Silver in COSMOS; a common daily multivitamin in PHS II) [PMID:35294969] [PMID:23162860] [PMID:37244291]. Essential Capsules differs: it has more vitamin E, iodine and B12, and less B6, and it adds non-nutrients. Multivitamin results are indirect evidence only.
- Vitamin D3: 2000 IU/d is exactly the VITAL dose [PMID:30415629].
- Vitamin E: product 67 mg d-alpha-tocopherol (about 100 IU). Trials covered 33-800 IU [PMID:37698992]. SELECT used 400 IU/d synthetic all-rac-alpha-tocopheryl acetate [PMID:21990298].
- NR: product 300 mg/d. Trials span 100-2000 mg/d. 300 mg/d raised NAD+ 51% [PMID:31278280]; 1000 mg/d was used in MCI [PMID:37994989] and Parkinson trials [PMID:42848371]; 2000 mg/d in obese men [PMID:29992272].
- Fisetin: product 100 mg/d. The only RCTs found used 200 mg/d for 12 weeks [PMID:42218768]. A registered trial tests 100 mg/d for 7 weeks [PMID:42633989]. Senolytic protocols use intermittent high doses; daily 100 mg has no outcome data.
- Ubiquinol: product 50 mg/d. The BP MA found larger effects below 200 mg/d [PMID:40495903].
- Spermidine: product 10 mg "as trihydrochloride"; the label does not say whether this is base or salt weight. Trial doses ranged from 0.9 mg/d (wheat germ) [PMID:35616942] to 15 mg/d [PMID:37111071] and 40 mg/d [PMID:39405978]. The 15 mg/d PK authors judged doses <15 mg/d unlikely to have short-term effects [PMID:37111071].
- Glucoraphanin: product 20 mg. Only the highest of 3 sprout-beverage doses changed benzene detoxication [PMID:31268126].
- Lithium: product 1 mg elemental/d. The MCI pilot used low-dose lithium carbonate [PMID:41770546]. Supplemental orotate doses are 5.5-67 times below therapeutic lithium [PMID:34146638].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Whole product: no published safety data. Blueprint states third-party heavy-metal testing; this was not independently verified here [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essentials-capsules].
- Multivitamins: no safety concerns in COSMOS [PMID:35294969]. USPSTF found limited evidence of serious harms for specific nutrients: hemorrhagic stroke (vitamin E), kidney stones (vitamin C, calcium), and hip fracture (vitamin A) [PMID:35727272].
- Vitamin E (67 mg = 447% DV): MA of 9 RCTs: hemorrhagic stroke RR 1.22 (1.00-1.48), ischemic stroke RR 0.90 [PMID:21051774]. A 2024 MA found the hemorrhagic excess when E was combined with other antioxidants (RR 1.22) [PMID:37698992]. SELECT: 400 IU/d raised prostate cancer risk (HR 1.17) [PMID:21990298]. These were mostly higher doses than in this product.
- Vitamin B6 (1.4 mg): Dutch pharmacovigilance found neuropathy reports from products with 1.4-100 mg per tablet; doses <50 mg/d "cannot be excluded" [PMID:29737502]. Low-dose reports continued after the Netherlands capped B6 at 21 mg/d [PMID:39888171]. Case report: B6 toxicity with a multivitamin providing only 6 mg/d [PMID:38098895]. The risk at 1.4 mg alone is likely negligible. Cumulative intake from other products is the concern.
- Zinc 15 mg: long-term zinc supplementation can cause copper deficiency (myelopathy, anemia, neutropenia) by inducing enterocyte metallothionein [PMID:42757636] [PMID:40866286] [PMID:29959467]. Hematological changes reverse with copper; neurological deficits only partly reverse [PMID:29959467]. The product contains no copper.
- Selenium 28 mcg: supplementation did not prevent cancer (RR 1.01, high certainty). RCTs showed slightly more type 2 diabetes, plus alopecia and dermatitis in SELECT [PMID:29376219]. The Danish PRECISE trial suggested a U-shaped glucose response [PMID:30280459]. 28 mcg is a low dose, so risk comes mainly from stacking.
- Iodine 200 mcg (133-136% DV per label): excess iodine is usually tolerated. Susceptible people (pre-existing thyroid disease, previous iodine deficiency) may develop hypo- or hyperthyroidism or autoimmunity at intakes only slightly above need [PMID:30891786] [PMID:38870258].
- Lithium 1 mg: lithium orotate was not genotoxic; rat NOAEL was 400 mg/kg/day [PMID:34146638]. Overdose of an internet orotate supplement caused mild toxicity (serum 0.40 mEq/L) [PMID:18072162]. In the MCI pilot of low-dose carbonate, diarrhea (29% vs 15%) and tremor (24% vs 15%) were more frequent [PMID:41770546].
- NR: 100-1000 mg/d for 8 weeks caused no flushing and no adverse-event excess; LDL was not raised [PMID:31278280]. NOPARK (1000 mg/d, 52 weeks, early Parkinson disease): worse MDS-UPDRS than placebo (MD 2.72 points, CI 0.47-4.98) and worse non-motor burden. Serious AEs were fewer with NR (8.3% vs 14.1%) [PMID:42848371].
- Spermidine 40 mg/d for 28 days caused no safety-lab changes [PMID:39405978].
- Probiotic: Lactobacillus infections (bacteremia, endocarditis) are rare and occur mostly in immunocompromised or diabetic patients or those with valve prostheses. L. acidophilus accounted for 5 of 48 cases in 2019-2021 [PMID:35334835].
- Glucoraphanin/sulforaphane: a 12-week broccoli sprout beverage did not change TSH, fT4, thyroglobulin or thyroid autoimmunity [PMID:30735751].

** Contraindications
- No product-level contraindication data. The following are ingredient-based.
- Current prescription lithium therapy: adds lithium in a narrow-therapeutic-index setting [PMID:8521679] [PMID:41620355].
- Pre-existing thyroid disease, especially autoimmune thyroiditis or nodular goiter, when total iodine intake is high [PMID:30891786] [PMID:38870258].
- Severe immunodeficiency or prosthetic heart valves (live probiotic): caution [PMID:35334835].
- Parkinson disease: NR 1 g/d worsened clinical scores in NOPARK. The product dose is 300 mg, so relevance is uncertain [PMID:42848371].
- Pregnancy/lactation and children: no data for lithium, fisetin, luteolin, spermidine or NR at these doses. Avoidance is a precaution, not evidence-based.

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Lithium carbonate/citrate: additive lithium. Thiazides (+25-40% serum lithium), NSAIDs and ACE inhibitors reduce lithium clearance [PMID:8521679] [PMID:41620355]. At 1 mg/d the added exposure is small, but it is unmonitored.
- Levothyroxine: calcium reduced levothyroxine absorption by 20-25% at 500 mg elemental calcium [PMID:21595516]. The product's 50 mg is a tenth of that. Separating doses is still advised for calcium and iron supplements [PMID:33801406]. Iodine and selenium can also shift thyroid tests [PMID:38870258] [PMID:38243784].
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets (warfarin, aspirin): vitamin E is linked to hemorrhagic stroke [PMID:21051774] [PMID:37698992]. CoQ10 100 mg/d did not change INR or warfarin dose in a crossover RCT [PMID:12772396].
- Statins: CoQ10 did not significantly improve statin myalgia [PMID:25440725]. No harmful interaction.
- Antithyroid drugs/amiodarone: iodine adds to iodine load [PMID:38870258].
- OATP substrates: luteolin inhibits OATP1B1/2B1 in vitro. Human relevance is unknown [PMID:38088265].
*** Other supplements
- Duplication is the main practical risk. Overlapping corpus items: Lithium, Zinc, Selenium, Iodine (potassium iodide), Vitamin B6, B Complex, Vitamin D-3, Vitamin E, Nicotinamide Riboside, NMN, Fisetin, CoQ10, Spermidine, Sulforaphane Glucosinolate (BroccoMax) and Boron.
- Zinc + Zinc: cumulative zinc raises copper-deficiency risk [PMID:42757636] [PMID:29959467].
- Selenium + Selenium: cumulative selenium; diabetes, alopecia and dermatitis signals in RCTs [PMID:29376219].
- Iodine + Iodine (potassium iodide): cumulative iodine above need [PMID:30891786].
- Vitamin B6 / B Complex: cumulative B6 and neuropathy reports at low doses [PMID:29737502] [PMID:38098895].
- Vitamin E + Vitamin E / EPA/DHA/DPA / Aspirin: possible bleeding stack; only vitamin E has outcome data [PMID:21051774].
- Vitamin D-3 + Vitamin D-3: 2000 IU already matches VITAL. Extra D adds no shown benefit [PMID:30415629] [PMID:31405892].
- Nicotinamide Riboside / NMN: same NAD+ pathway; dose stacking without clear benefit [PMID:31278280] [PMID:40275690].
- Boron + Boron: an acute 11.6 mg boron dose did not alter coagulation or glucose [PMID:12428176]; cumulative long-term intake is unstudied. Spermidine, Fisetin, CoQ10 and Sulforaphane Glucosinolate (BroccoMax) duplicates add dose without outcome data [PMID:37111071] [PMID:42633989] [PMID:40495903] [PMID:31268126].
*** Conditions
- Thyroid disease (iodine, selenium): see Contraindications [PMID:38870258] [PMID:38243784].
- Kidney impairment: lithium is cleared renally [PMID:8521679]. Creatinine rises were similar on low-dose lithium and placebo in the MCI pilot [PMID:41770546].
- Parkinson disease (NR) [PMID:42848371]; immunocompromise (probiotic) [PMID:35334835].
- Prostate cancer risk: SELECT signals for vitamin E (and selenium at high baseline status) [PMID:21990298] [PMID:29376219].
*** Lab-test interference
- Biotin interferes with streptavidin-biotin immunoassays: falsely low hs-troponin T and TSH, and falsely high T3 and vitamin D [PMID:30296442]. Studies model milligram-level intakes (for example, 300 mg) [PMID:31944861]. The 50 mcg here is unlikely to interfere alone but adds to other biotin-containing products.
- True physiological changes, not artifacts: higher 25(OH)D [PMID:35026158], lower TSH/TPOAb with selenium in Hashimoto [PMID:38243784], and possibly serum lithium if measured [PMID:18072162].

** Open questions
- No trial of the combined formula exists. Its efficacy, tolerability and possible interactions among its 24 actives are unknown.
- Whether multivitamin cognitive and epigenetic-clock benefits from Centrum Silver transfer to this composition.
- Clinical relevance of 1 mg/d lithium. Ecological suicide/dementia associations and mouse data have not been tested in adequately powered human trials at trace doses.
- Daily 100 mg fisetin and 100 mg luteolin: bioavailability from these extracts and long-term safety are unstudied.
- Why NR worsened Parkinson outcomes in NOPARK, and whether this applies to lower doses or to people without Parkinson disease.
- Label consistency (iodide vs iodate, calcium source, DSLD biotin unit) and lot-level verification of the stated amounts.
- Long-term copper status with 15 mg zinc and no copper.
