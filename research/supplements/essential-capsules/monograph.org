** Identity & forms
- A multi-ingredient branded dietary supplement, not a single nutrient. Blueprint’s current product page describes 23 longevity actives, including ubiquinol, fisetin, luteolin, lithium orotate, Lactobacillus acidophilus and B vitamins; formulation/label can change, so the physical lot label governs ingredient identity [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/en-gb/collections/daily-health-longevity/products/essentials-capsules]. A 2025 NIH DSLD label record describes 24 actives; differences underscore version control [URL:https://hellopharmacist.com/drug-supplement-interactions/supplements/essential-capsules-by-blueprint-bryan-johnson-2431781].

** Mechanisms
- The formula combines micronutrients with unrelated compounds, so no single mechanism can be assigned. For example, B vitamins serve as metabolic cofactors while ubiquinol participates in electron transport; the product has no published mechanism validated as a whole [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essentials-capsules].

** Dosing in trials
- Current product page recommends 2 capsules daily with food. The catalog/label has changed over time; identify version, amounts and serving size from the bottle before extrapolating [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/en-gb/collections/daily-health-longevity/products/essentials-capsules; URL:https://hellopharmacist.com/drug-supplement-interactions/supplements/essential-capsules-by-blueprint-bryan-johnson-2431781].

** Safety & adverse effects
- A multi-ingredient formula inherits ingredient-specific risks; label record includes vitamins/minerals, lithium orotate, nicotinamide riboside, botanical/polyphenol compounds and probiotic [URL:https://hellopharmacist.com/drug-supplement-interactions/supplements/essential-capsules-by-blueprint-bryan-johnson-2431781]. Supplemental biotin can interfere with certain immunoassays (including troponin and thyroid tests); FDA warns of falsely low troponin results [URL:https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications/update-fda-warns-biotin-may-interfere-lab-tests-fda-safety-communication].

** Contraindications
- No whole-product contraindication trials exist. Ingredient-level cautions apply: lithium products with renal impairment/dehydration and interacting medicines; iodine in thyroid disease; calcium/minerals with absorption-sensitive drugs; vitamin E with bleeding risk; check the current label and NIH ODS ingredient fact sheets [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/list-all/].

** Interactions
- Potential interaction burden includes vitamin E with anticoagulants/antiplatelets, calcium with levothyroxine/quinolone/tetracycline absorption, iodine with thyroid-active drugs, and minerals with integrase inhibitors. Biotin can interfere with lab immunoassays. These are constituent-level risks, not evidence of a tested product interaction [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/list-all/; URL:https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications/update-fda-warns-biotin-may-interfere-lab-tests-fda-safety-communication].

** Genotype notes

** Latest research

No new product-level efficacy RCT found in PubMed/product source search (through 2026-10-13).

** Open questions
