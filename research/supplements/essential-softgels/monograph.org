** Identity & forms
- Historical Blueprint product name for the formula now called Advanced Antioxidants. Official page says it contains lutein, zeaxanthin, lycopene, astaxanthin, vitamin K1, K2-MK4 and K2-MK7 in a delayed-release capsule; the old softgel delivery changed [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel].

** Mechanisms
- Lutein and zeaxanthin accumulate in the macula; vitamin K supports gamma-carboxylation of coagulation proteins and bone-associated proteins. Those ingredient mechanisms do not establish efficacy of the product’s exact doses or its combined formula [PMID:23644932; URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel].

** Dosing in trials
- Current formula page recommends one capsule daily with food and reports 30 servings per container. Current delayed-release capsule is not identical to the former softgel; follow the current label, not historical serving assumptions [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Product includes vitamin K forms and carotenoids; vitamin K can antagonize warfarin. Carotenoids can cause benign skin discoloration at high intake; supplement beta-carotene raises harm in smokers in other formulations, but this product page does not list beta-carotene [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel; URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/].

** Contraindications
- Warfarin use is a major formulation-specific concern because vitamin K intake changes can alter INR; avoid assuming compatibility. Allergy to capsule/excipients is product-specific [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/].

** Interactions
- Vitamin K1/K2 can reduce warfarin effect; maintain consistent intake and monitor INR when intake changes [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/]. Fat-containing meal may aid absorption of fat-soluble components, but no product interaction trial is reported [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source specific to Essential Softgels was identified in this search; no genotype claim is made.

** Latest research
- Official page now calls this Advanced Antioxidants and states the only formula change was delivery from softgel to delayed-release capsule; lists seven fat-soluble actives and one-capsule daily use [URL:https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/products/essential-softgel]. No product-level RCT located; formula-level human outcomes remain unestablished.

** Open questions
- Larger, better reported human studies with standardized products, clinically meaningful endpoints and longer follow-up are needed; current gaps are noted above.
