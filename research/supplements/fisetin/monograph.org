** Identity & forms
- Fisetin is a plant flavonol found in strawberries and other produce; concentrated supplements are marketed for senolytic effects. [PMID:42796982]

** Mechanisms
- Proposed biological activity and the best-established physiological context are described above; clinical mechanisms remain uncertain where outcome evidence is limited. ["PMID:39434114"]

| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | Certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary studied clinical/biomarker outcomes | Systematic review/meta-analysis or RCT synthesis | Senolytic, antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects are primarily preclinical | See narrative | low | PMID:39434114; PMID:42796982 |
| Long-term clinical outcomes | Evidence insufficient or mixed | No established benefit | Not established | very low | PMID:39434114; PMID:42796982 |

** Dosing in trials
- No established evidence-based dose; research protocols vary and no validated OTC regimen exists. [PMID:42796982]

** Safety & adverse effects
- Human long-term safety and pharmacokinetics are inadequately characterized; potential drug-metabolism interactions remain theoretical. [PMID:42796982]

** Contraindications
- No clinical contraindication set established; avoid treating preclinical hypotheses as proven therapy. [PMID:42796982]

** Interactions
- Potential interactions with anticoagulants or drug-metabolizing enzymes are theoretical; clinical interaction studies are lacking. [PMID:42796982]

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed; no genotype-specific claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2025–2026 literature: 2024 phase 2 STOP-Sepsis protocol describes testing fisetin in hospitalized older adults; protocol reports no efficacy result. [PMID:39434114]

** Open questions
- Whether findings translate into durable patient-important outcomes, optimal formulation/dose, and long-term safety remains uncertain. [PMID:39434114; PMID:42796982]
