** Identity & forms
- Ginger root is rhizome of Zingiber officinale, used as food, powder, extracts and standardized gingerols/shogaols. [PMID:38261398]

** Mechanisms
- Proposed biological activity and the best-established physiological context are described above; clinical mechanisms remain uncertain where outcome evidence is limited. ["PMID:38261398"]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | Certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary studied clinical/biomarker outcomes | Systematic review/meta-analysis or RCT synthesis | Meta-analysis of 27 RCTs (n=1,309) found small changes in weight (−1.52 kg) and BMI (−0.58 kg/m²), with low/very-low certainty | See narrative | low | PMID:38261398; PMID:41123858 |
| Long-term clinical outcomes | Evidence insufficient or mixed | No established benefit | Not established | very low | PMID:38261398; PMID:41123858 |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials often used 1.2–2 g/day powder for 4–12 weeks; effective body-mass dose-response estimate ~2 g/day. [PMID:39053695] [PMID:38261398]

** Safety & adverse effects
- Heartburn, abdominal discomfort, diarrhea and mouth/throat irritation; supplement safety data in pregnancy less robust than food use. [PMID:39343171]

** Contraindications
- Caution with gallstone disease and bleeding-risk medicines; pregnancy evidence is specific to nausea and products/doses. [PMID:39343171]

** Interactions
- Possible additive antiplatelet effect; may lower glucose or blood pressure, so monitor when combined with corresponding drugs (interaction evidence limited). [PMID:41123858]

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed; no genotype-specific claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2025–2026 literature: 2025 dose-response meta-analysis of 29 RCTs evaluated oxidative and inflammatory biomarkers. [PMID:41123858]

** Open questions
- Whether findings translate into durable patient-important outcomes, optimal formulation/dose, and long-term safety remains uncertain. [PMID:38261398; PMID:41123858]
