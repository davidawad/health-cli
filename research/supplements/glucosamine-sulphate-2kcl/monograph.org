** Identity & forms
- Glucosamine sulfate 2KCl is a crystalline glucosamine sulfate formulation stabilized with potassium chloride; products and salt forms differ. [PMID:38581640]

** Mechanisms
- Proposed biological activity and the best-established physiological context are described above; clinical mechanisms remain uncertain where outcome evidence is limited. ["PMID:38581640"]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | Certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary studied clinical/biomarker outcomes | Systematic review/meta-analysis or RCT synthesis | 2024 review of 25 RCTs found structural joint-space signal for glucosamine sulfate but not consistent symptom benefit; 2025 network review included heterogeneous SYSADOAs and concluded pooled pain/function benefit | See narrative | low | PMID:38581640; PMID:40425393 |
| Long-term clinical outcomes | Evidence insufficient or mixed | No established benefit | Not established | very low | PMID:38581640; PMID:40425393 |

** Dosing in trials
- Common studied dose is 1,500 mg/day oral glucosamine sulfate, typically for months; formulations are not dose-equivalent by label mass. [PMID:38581640]

** Safety & adverse effects
- Usually mild GI effects; allergy concern with shellfish-derived products; safety review generally favorable. [PMID:38581640]

** Contraindications
- Caution in shellfish allergy depending on source and with diabetes due to glucose monitoring uncertainty. [PMID:38581640]

** Interactions
- Warfarin interaction reports indicate increased INR/bleeding; monitor INR. [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/glucosamine-and-chondroitin-for-osteoarthritis]

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed; no genotype-specific claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2025–2026 literature: 2025 network meta-analysis searched through January 2025 and assessed supplement interventions for OA. [PMID:40425393]

** Open questions
- Whether findings translate into durable patient-important outcomes, optimal formulation/dose, and long-term safety remains uncertain. [PMID:38581640; PMID:40425393]
