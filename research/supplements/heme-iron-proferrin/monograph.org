** Identity & forms
- Proferrin is a branded heme iron polypeptide (HIP), a bovine hemoglobin-derived iron product; products and elemental iron content vary [PMID:31944128].
** Mechanisms
- Provides heme-bound iron for absorption and hemoglobin synthesis; comparative superiority over ferrous salts is not established [PMID:31944128].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials have tested varied elemental iron quantities; follow product label and trial indication; dosing cannot be inferred across HIP formulations [PMID:31944128].
** Safety & adverse effects
- GI symptoms and iron overload are concerns; accidental overdose can be fatal in children. Long-term HIP-specific safety evidence limited [PMID:31944128].
** Contraindications
- Iron overload/hemochromatosis and iron-replete states; identify deficiency before prolonged use.
** Interactions
- Iron decreases absorption of levothyroxine, tetracyclines, quinolones and bisphosphonates; separate dosing per drug labeling. Vitamin C can enhance nonheme iron absorption, less clearly relevant to heme iron.

** Latest research
- 2025-11: 2025 RCT of heme iron polypeptide (Proferrin) versus ferrous salts in Gambian children did not show Hb advantage; pediatric setting limits extrapolation [PMID:41300263].
