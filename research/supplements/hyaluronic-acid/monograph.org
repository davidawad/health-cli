** Identity & forms
- Glycosaminoglycan present in extracellular matrix; oral supplements, topical products, and prescription intra-articular formulations are distinct interventions. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Mechanisms
- Binds water and contributes to extracellular matrix viscosity and tissue hydration; oral absorption/metabolism is not equivalent to local injection. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]

** Dosing in trials
- Oral skin trials commonly use roughly 120-240 mg/day for 4-12 weeks; products and molecular weights differ. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Oral trials report few short-term adverse events; long-term safety and source-related allergy risks poorly studied. Injection has separate local risks. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Contraindications
- Caution with allergy to source material or product excipients; pregnancy/lactation evidence insufficient. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Interactions
- No established oral drug interactions. Do not conflate supplement with intra-articular injection; no lab-test interference established. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Genotype notes
- No genotype evidence identified.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: 2025 meta-analysis of oral HA skin RCTs reported positive pooled outcomes but called for larger robust trials [PMID:40911749]. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
** Open questions
- Bioavailability, optimal molecular weight/dose, and clinical relevance of cosmetic-scale changes remain uncertain. [PMID:40911749] [PMID:38009035]
