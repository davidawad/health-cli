** Identity & forms
- Inulin is a β(2→1)-linked fructan, often extracted from chicory root; chain length distinguishes shorter oligofructose from longer inulin. It resists human digestive enzymes and is fermented in the colon [PMID:38309832].

** Mechanisms
- Inulin-type fructans are nondigestible fermentable carbohydrates. Microbial fermentation yields short-chain fatty acids and can alter microbiota composition; this plausibility does not establish meaningful clinical benefit [PMID:38309832].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---------+--------------------+-----------+-------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| Weight/body composition | SR/MA, 32 studies | Small reduction | weight MD −0.97 kg; BMI −0.39 kg/m²; waist −1.03 cm | low | [PMID:39313030] |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials use roughly 3–30 g/day inulin-type fructans for 2 weeks to several months. Weight meta-analysis found modest outcomes with varied dose/duration; no clear optimal dose established [PMID:39313030; PMID:38309832].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Common dose-related effects are gas, bloating, abdominal pain and altered stool due to fermentation; gradual titration is common in trials. Severe intolerance may occur in people with IBS/FODMAP sensitivity [PMID:38309832].

** Contraindications
- Avoid in known inulin/chicory allergy. Inulin may aggravate symptoms in people with fructan-sensitive IBS; tolerance is dose-dependent [PMID:38309832].

** Interactions
- No established drug interaction or assay interference. Like other fiber, large co-ingestion could alter absorption timing of oral medicines, but direct inulin interaction evidence is insufficient [PMID:38309832].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source specific to inulin was identified in this search; no genotype claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of 55 RCTs (n=2,518) found small LDL, triglyceride and weight reductions with low/very-low certainty [PMID:38309832]. 2024 weight meta-analysis covering 32 studies estimated −0.97 kg versus controls; erratum/corrigendum was published in 2025 [PMID:39313030; PMID:40414749].

** Open questions
- Larger, better reported human studies with standardized products, clinically meaningful endpoints and longer follow-up are needed; current gaps are noted above.
