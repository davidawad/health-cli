** Identity & forms
- MK-7 is a long-chain menaquinone form of vitamin K found in fermented foods and supplements [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/].
** Mechanisms
- Vitamin K is a cofactor for gamma-carboxylation of osteocalcin and matrix Gla protein, implicated in bone and vascular biology [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials commonly used 180 mcg/day MK-7 for 3 years; no optimal dose established [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/].
** Safety & adverse effects
- No established UL; main clinically important risk is antagonism of warfarin/VKAs.
** Contraindications
- Warfarin/VKA; maintain consistent K intake and INR monitoring.
