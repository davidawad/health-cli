** Identity & forms
- Conditionally essential aromatic amino acid found in foods and supplement powders/capsules. [PMID:26126245]
** Mechanisms
- Precursor to dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine, and thyroid hormones; conversion depends on enzyme activity and substrate availability. [PMID:26126245]
** Evidence summary by outcome

|---------+--------------------+-----------+-------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| Cognitive performance under acute stress | Rapid evidence assessment included 10 RCTs and 4 controlled studies; evidence was insufficient for confident recommendations, with heterogeneous small experiments | Possible / uncertain | Rapid evidence assessment included 10 RCTs and 4 controlled studies; evidence was insufficient for confident recommendations, with heterogeneous small experiments. | low | [PMID:26126245] |
** Dosing in trials
- Acute studies commonly used 100-150 mg/kg or fixed gram doses before cold, sleep deprivation, or cognitive stress; no chronic dose established. [PMID:26126245]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Possible nausea, headache, restlessness; safety data for prolonged high-dose use limited. [PMID:26126245]
** Contraindications
- Avoid unsupervised use with hyperthyroidism or thyroid hormone treatment; phenylketonuria management is specialist-led. [PMID:26126245]
** Interactions
- Potential additive effects with levodopa and thyroid hormone; monoamine oxidase inhibitor interaction concerns lack robust supplement trial data. Separate from levodopa per drug-specific advice. [PMID:26126245]
** Genotype notes
- No genotype-based supplementation evidence located.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: No compelling new large human efficacy trial identified in the last 24 months; 2015 evidence assessment remains a key synthesis [PMID:26126245]. [PMID:26126245]
** Open questions
- Which stress contexts, if any, produce reproducible benefit remains unclear. [PMID:26126245]
