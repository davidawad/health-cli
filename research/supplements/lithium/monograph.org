** Identity & forms
- Lithium is a monovalent alkali metal; prescription lithium carbonate is an established mood stabilizer, while OTC low-dose forms have no approved disease-treatment indication [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/search.cfm?query=lithium%20carbonate].
** Mechanisms
- At therapeutic exposure lithium modulates intracellular signaling including GSK-3; neuroprotective hypotheses remain under study [PMID:38253184].

** Dosing in trials
- MCI pilot used daily low-dose lithium carbonate, individualized titration, 2 years; investigational doses should not be generalized to OTC lithium salts [URL:https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2845746].
** Safety & adverse effects
- Narrow therapeutic index at prescription doses; tremor, GI symptoms, renal and thyroid effects, toxicity with dehydration/renal impairment [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/search.cfm?query=lithium%20carbonate].
** Contraindications
- Renal disease, dehydration/sodium depletion, pregnancy considerations; prescription label monitoring is required.
** Interactions
- NSAIDs, thiazide diuretics, ACE inhibitors/ARBs can raise lithium; monitor lithium, kidney and thyroid function [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/search.cfm?query=lithium%20carbonate].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic/nutrigenomic source was identified for a supplement-specific genotype effect; no genotype claim made.
** Latest research
- 2026 MCI pilot: none of six coprimary endpoints statistically significant; modest, exploratory memory difference and balanced serious adverse events [URL:https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2845746].
** Open questions
- Which formulations, populations, and durations produce clinically meaningful outcomes rather than biomarker-only changes?
- Are there long-term benefits or harms beyond the relatively short human trials available?
