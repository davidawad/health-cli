** Identity & forms
- Lysine is an essential amino acid obtained from diet; supplements generally provide L-lysine or its hydrochloride salt [PMID:30855987].
** Mechanisms
- Protein synthesis and competition with arginine for viral replication have been proposed to explain HSV effects; mechanistic rationale is not clinical proof [PMID:30855987].

** Dosing in trials
- HSV studies used roughly 1–3 g/day; no validated treatment regimen or established optimal duration [PMID:30855987].
** Safety & adverse effects
- Short-term studies commonly report few effects; high doses can cause GI symptoms. Long-term safety data and renal effects are insufficiently characterized [PMID:30855987].
** Contraindications
- Caution with significant kidney disease; avoid assuming supplements replace antiviral treatment.
** Interactions
- No established clinically proven drug interaction; high-dose amino acid supplementation may affect amino acid balance.
