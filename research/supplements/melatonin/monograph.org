** Identity & forms
- Endogenous circadian hormone sold as OTC supplement in some countries; product quality and regulatory status vary. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]
** Mechanisms
- Acts at MT1/MT2 receptors to signal circadian timing; timing relative to circadian phase is important. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]

** Dosing in trials
- Trials commonly used 0.5-5 mg near bedtime; dose/timing differs for circadian disorders and jet lag. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Sleepiness, headache, dizziness, vivid dreams; supplement content may differ from label. Long-term safety evidence limited. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]
** Contraindications
- Caution in pregnancy/lactation, seizure disorders, and conditions/medications affected by sedation or anticoagulation. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]
** Interactions
- Fluvoxamine (CYP1A2 inhibition) can markedly raise melatonin exposure; possible warfarin interaction reports; additive sedation with CNS depressants [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]. [PMID:28364495] [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches] [URL:https://www.fda.gov/media/169557/download]
** Genotype notes
- No established pharmacogenomic dosing recommendation.
