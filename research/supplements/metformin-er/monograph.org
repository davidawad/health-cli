** Identity & forms
- Prescription biguanide antihyperglycemic, extended-release oral formulation; not a dietary supplement [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=7cb47c3e-6657-4c11-8aa2-ae172e5a8529].
** Mechanisms
- Reduces hepatic glucose production and improves insulin sensitivity; exact molecular mechanism is not fully established [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=7cb47c3e-6657-4c11-8aa2-ae172e5a8529].

** Dosing in trials
- ER adult label starts 500 mg once daily with evening meal, titrates weekly as tolerated, max commonly 2000 mg/day depending product labeling [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=7cb47c3e-6657-4c11-8aa2-ae172e5a8529].
** Safety & adverse effects
- GI effects common; boxed warning lactic acidosis. Label contraindicates eGFR <30, hypersensitivity, metabolic acidosis; can lower B12 [same DailyMed URL].
** Contraindications
- Severe renal impairment, metabolic acidosis/DKA, hypersensitivity; assess renal and hepatic status.
** Interactions
- Alcohol and carbonic anhydrase inhibitors raise lactic acidosis risk; cationic drugs that reduce clearance (ranolazine, dolutegravir, cimetidine, vandetanib); insulin/secretagogues increase hypoglycemia risk [same DailyMed URL].
- Structured high-priority interactions are represented in interactions.json; absence of an edge is not proof of no interaction.
** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic/nutrigenomic source was identified for a supplement-specific genotype effect; no genotype claim made.
** Latest research
- 2025-02: 2025 ER label continues to specify B12 monitoring and renal contraindications; no recent ER-specific outcome evidence was opened [same DailyMed URL].
** Open questions
- Which formulations, populations, and durations produce clinically meaningful outcomes rather than biomarker-only changes?
- Are there long-term benefits or harms beyond the relatively short human trials available?
