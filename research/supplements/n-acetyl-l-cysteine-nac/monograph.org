** Identity & forms
- Acetylated cysteine prodrug. It started as a mucolytic about 60 years ago, is now standard care for paracetamol (acetaminophen) poisoning, and is on the WHO essential medicines list [PMID:34171332].
- Regulated US drug forms: Acetadote IV (acetaminophen antidote, 300 mg/kg total) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=472f158a-5ab9-4308-8e49-1116e6ea3d39]; acetylcysteine solution as an inhaled mucolytic or oral antidote (140 mg/kg load, then 70 mg/kg every 4 h for 17 doses) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5558a5f5-e821-473b-7d8a-5d33d09f0586].
- US supplement status: FDA holds that NAC is excluded from the dietary-supplement definition. In 2022 it denied the CRN/NPA citizen petitions but chose enforcement discretion for NAC products labeled as supplements. FDA reported no safety concerns found in its review to date [URL:https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-issues-response-two-citizen-petitions-nac-dietary-supplements].
- Oral PK: peak 0.35-4 mg/L at 1-2 h after 200-400 mg; Vd 0.33-0.47 L/kg; terminal half-life about 6.25 h; excreted mainly as inorganic sulphate [PMID:2029805]. Bioavailability varies by formulation, and clearance falls in advanced CKD [PMID:39430194].
- GlyNAC is glycine plus NAC, used in ageing trials [PMID:35975308] [PMID:34587244]. In mice, GlyNAC extended lifespan by 24% [PMID:35268089]. This monograph covers NAC alone. The combined product "NAC, Ginger, Curcumin" has its own monograph.

** Mechanisms
- Three conventional mechanisms: (i) reducing disulfide bonds, (ii) directly scavenging ROS, (iii) supplying cysteine for glutathione (GSH) synthesis. A 2021 review argues these do not explain most effects, and proposes conversion of NAC to H2S and sulfane-sulfur species instead [PMID:34171332].
- Airway: lowers MUC5AC/MUC5B expression and goblet-cell hyperplasia in vitro and in animals, so it acts as both mucolytic and mucoregulator. Human bronchial-tissue data are lacking [PMID:39493366].
- Paracetamol antidote: replenishes GSH to detoxify the metabolite NAPQI [PMID:29473717].
- Glutamate modulation is the rationale for psychiatric and addiction use [PMID:39556483] [PMID:39376972].
- Thiol donor: potentiates organic-nitrate vasodilation [PMID:3079676] [PMID:8205670].
- Lowers activity of coagulation factors II, VII, IX and X [PMID:39878294]. Inhibits ADP-induced platelet aggregation [PMID:16607076] [PMID:21600014].
- Redox signalling blunting: in muscle, NAC blunted the post-exercise activation of mTOR/p70S6K/p38 and delayed recovery [PMID:23719546]. In mouse models, NAC (with vitamin E) sped lung-tumour progression through the ROS-p53 axis [PMID:24477002].

** Dosing in trials
- COPD: 600 mg/d up to >=1,200 mg/d. In one MA, low dose (<=600 mg/d) reduced exacerbations (RR 0.84) and high dose did not (RR 0.88, NS) [PMID:42473447]. An older MA favoured >=1,200 mg/d when airflow obstruction is present [PMID:26324807].
- Psychiatry: 1,000-3,000 mg/d for 8-24 weeks in depression MAs [PMID:39504621]. Cannabis/AUD youth trials used 1,200 mg twice daily [PMID:39910268] [PMID:41016625]. The PTSD/AUD trial used 2,400 mg/d [PMID:40875537]. Schizophrenia benefit appeared only at >=24 weeks [PMID:31826654].
- Influenza-like illness: 600 mg twice daily for 6 months [PMID:9230243]. IPF trials: 600 mg three times daily [PMID:36514019].
- PCOS ovulation induction: 1.8 g/d [PMID:39415242].
- GlyNAC: 2.4-7.2 g/d (glycine:NAC 1:1) for 2 weeks [PMID:35821844]; 16-24 weeks in Sekhar trials [PMID:35975308] [PMID:33783984].
- Contrast prophylaxis (ineffective): 1,200 mg twice daily around the procedure [PMID:21859972]; 5 days oral in PRESERVE [PMID:29130810].
- Paracetamol overdose (medical only): IV 3-bag 150/50/100 mg/kg over 21 h, or 2-bag 200/100 mg/kg over 20 h [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=472f158a-5ab9-4308-8e49-1116e6ea3d39]; 12-h SARPO regimen [PMID:40414507].
- Sport: typical 5.8 g/d (range 1.2-20 g/d). Side effects tended to rise with dose [PMID:28102488].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Oral: adverse events similar to placebo in COPD and psychiatric MAs [PMID:42473447] [PMID:29457216]. In a youth trial at 2,400 mg/d, GI events were more common on NAC (63/98 vs 37/94) [PMID:39910268]. Nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea are the usual effects [PMID:2029805].
- IV anaphylactoid (non-IgE) reactions: 8.2% of 6,455 courses, 75% cutaneous, 95% within the first 5 h [PMID:29423816]; up to 17% in the label's loading-dose study, anaphylaxis 0.1-0.2% [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=472f158a-5ab9-4308-8e49-1116e6ea3d39].
  - Risk factors: female sex and asthma/atopy [PMID:19280424]. Fewer reactions with 2-bag/SNAP regimens and with high paracetamol levels [PMID:31601129] [PMID:40013897] [PMID:40850738].
- Inhaled: bronchospasm in asthmatics; stop if it progresses. Also stomatitis, rhinorrhoea, nausea [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5558a5f5-e821-473b-7d8a-5d33d09f0586].
- IV fluid overload can cause hyponatraemia and seizures, mainly in patients under 40 kg [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=472f158a-5ab9-4308-8e49-1116e6ea3d39].
- Haemostasis: PT/INR rises 15-30% [PMID:39878294]. Platelet inhibition and greater blood loss with IV NAC in aortic surgery [PMID:16607076].
- Cancer signal (preclinical only): in mice, NAC accelerated lung tumours [PMID:24477002] and induced lung adenocarcinoma in aged/JunD-deficient mice [PMID:31578304]. No human trial evidence.
- Exercise: blunted muscle repair signalling and recovery after eccentric exercise (n=10) [PMID:23719546].
- IPF: triple therapy (prednisone + azathioprine + NAC) raised death (8 vs 1) and hospitalisation [PMID:22607134].

** Contraindications
- Prior anaphylactoid reaction to acetylcysteine. Asthma or bronchospasm needs close monitoring, especially with inhaled or IV forms [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5558a5f5-e821-473b-7d8a-5d33d09f0586] [PMID:19280424].
- Suspected paracetamol overdose needs emergency, protocol-based treatment. Supplement NAC is not a substitute [PMID:29473717].
- Caution with bleeding disorders or around major surgery [PMID:16607076] [PMID:39878294].
- Caution in people with lung cancer or at high risk of it, such as heavy smokers (preclinical signal) [PMID:24477002] [PMID:31578304].
- Pregnancy and lactation: PK data are lacking [PMID:2029805]. The Acetadote label reports limited human data and no adverse fetal effects in animals [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=472f158a-5ab9-4308-8e49-1116e6ea3d39].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Nitroglycerin and other organic nitrates: NAC potentiates nitrate headache and temporal-artery dilation [PMID:1505148] and coronary dilation [PMID:3079676]. It also potentiates the haemodynamic effects of isosorbide dinitrate [PMID:8205670] [PMID:1714011].
  In unstable angina, combining NAC with nitroglycerin caused intolerable headache in 35% of patients [PMID:9120179].
- Activated charcoal: lowered oral NAC peak by 29% and AUC by 39% [PMID:3663288]. The label advises lavage before oral NAC if charcoal was given [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5558a5f5-e821-473b-7d8a-5d33d09f0586].
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets (warfarin, aspirin): NAC has factor-lowering and platelet-inhibiting effects [PMID:16607076] [PMID:21600014] [PMID:39878294]. Additive bleeding risk is plausible but not studied clinically.
- Prednisone + azathioprine (IPF): harmful as a triple regimen [PMID:22607134].
- Metformin: compared head-to-head with NAC in PCOS (similar pregnancy rates; NAC lowered BMI and testosterone). No combination interaction data [PMID:31749393] [PMID:34592079].
- Carbamazepine: no human interaction study was retrieved, so no claim is made.
*** Other supplements
- Glycine: combined as GlyNAC to supply both GSH precursors [PMID:35975308] [PMID:35821844].
- Vitamin E: in mice, the combination with NAC sped lung-cancer progression [PMID:24477002].
- Vitamin C: adding ascorbic acid to NAC gave no extra contrast-nephropathy protection [PMID:28452187]. "NAC, Ginger, Curcumin" (combined product): stacking it with NAC duplicates NAC dose. GI events rise with dose [PMID:28102488] [PMID:39910268].
*** Conditions
- Asthma: bronchospasm risk with inhaled NAC; higher anaphylactoid risk with IV NAC (see Contraindications).
- IPF: NAC gave no benefit overall [PMID:24836309]. The effect may depend on TOLLIP genotype (see Genotype notes).
- Endurance or strength training: NAC may blunt adaptation signalling [PMID:23719546]. It does not improve performance [PMID:28102488].
- CKD: NAC clearance falls in advanced CKD [PMID:39430194]. Bleeding risk or major surgery: see Contraindications.
*** Lab-test interference
- Creatinine: NAC lowers serum creatinine but not cystatin C. The effect is larger with non-Jaffe (enzymatic) assays (WMD -3.24 umol/L) and IV dosing (WMD -31.10 umol/L), which suggests analytic interference [PMID:33615065].
- Trinder (peroxidase) assays: >=10% inhibition at NAC levels of 570 mg/L (triglycerides), 740 mg/L (cholesterol), 790 mg/L (enzymatic creatinine), 1,100 mg/L (uric acid), 1,760 mg/L (HDL-C) and 2,900 mg/L (LDL-C) [PMID:26500003].
  These levels are above those expected with therapy, so effects are relevant mainly at high IV concentrations. Only minor creatinine inhibition was seen in patients [PMID:26500003].
- PT/INR: falsely raised by 15-30% during IV NAC, independent of liver injury [PMID:39878294] [PMID:34549665] [PMID:40663728].

** Genotype notes
- TOLLIP rs3750920 (IPF), post hoc PANTHER analysis replicated in a second cohort: NAC reduced the composite end point in TT carriers (HR 0.14, 0.02-0.83) but trended toward harm in CC carriers (HR 3.23, 0.79-13.16) [PMID:26331942].
- PRECISIONS is a prospective RCT testing 600 mg three times daily in TT carriers (about 25% of IPF patients) [PMID:36514019]. No nutrigenomic data were found for other indications.

** Latest research
- [2026-10] Bipolar-depression NMA (22 RCTs): NAC SMD -0.37 across 8 trials; low to very low certainty [PMID:42863801].
- [2026-08] MASLD RCT (n=60, 2,400 mg/d, 12 wk): no change in MDA, HOMA-IR, steatosis or fibrosis [PMID:42587287].
- [2026-07] COPD MA (14 RCTs, n=2,856): exacerbations RR 0.87; no FEV1/QoL benefit [PMID:42473447].
- [2026-05] AUD/PTSD SR (7 RCTs, n=566): no consistent benefit for craving or PTSD [PMID:42115982].
- [2026-04] Contrast-nephropathy MA (49 studies): neither oral nor IV NAC reduced AKI [PMID:42015439].
- [2026-03] Parkinson's SR (9 studies, n=196): preliminary motor/DAT-binding signals [PMID:41874704]. Neurology SR across 7 disorders: strongest signal in mild TBI [PMID:41977262].
- [2025-12] SNAP 12-h regimen: fewer anaphylactoid reactions at all ages [PMID:40850738].
- [2025-11] CA-AKI NMA (72 RCTs): hydration + oral NAC OR 0.78. This conflicts with the large null RCTs [PMID:41312470]. Post-ERCP pancreatitis MA: RR 0.66 (NS) [PMID:41188701].
- [2025-09] Youth AUD RCT (n=126): no overall effect; fewer drinks in moderate-severe AUD [PMID:41016625]. Cochrane cannabis review: NAC abstinence RR 1.17 (NS) [PMID:41025421]. Schizophrenia NMA: NAC 2,000 mg/d benefit in several domains [PMID:40945815].
- [2025-08] PTSD/AUD RCT (n=182): no better than placebo [PMID:40875537]. COVID-19 mortality MA: OR 0.49 [PMID:40728675].
- [2025-07] In vitro study: NAC raises INR by 0.039 per mmol/L [PMID:40663728].
- [2025-05] SARPO RCT: 12-h IV regimen non-inferior [PMID:40414507]. Cisplatin hearing-loss MA: null [PMID:40323204].
- [2025-03] Two-bag vs three-bag MA: adverse events OR 0.24 [PMID:40013897]. Male-infertility MAs: semen parameters improved [PMID:40126496] [PMID:40350672].
- [2025-02] Youth cannabis RCT (n=192): null without contingency management [PMID:39910268].
- [2025-01] PCOS MA (22 studies): higher progesterone and endometrial thickness [PMID:39861414]. INR review: 15-30% rise [PMID:39878294].
- [2024-12] Exercise MA (20 studies): less soreness and lactate [PMID:39632267].
- [2024-11] Depression MA: SMD -0.24 [PMID:39504621]. Craving MA: null, with possible publication bias [PMID:39556483].
- [2024-10] Mucolytics in stable COPD MA [PMID:39413571]; mucus-hypersecretion SR [PMID:39493366]; PCOS ovulation-induction RCT (n=230) [PMID:39415242].

** Open questions
- Can genotype (TOLLIP) or high oxidative-stress phenotypes identify NAC responders in IPF, COPD and ageing? PRECISIONS results are pending.
- Do GlyNAC findings replicate in larger, independent, longer RCTs with clinical end points?
- What is the optimal COPD dose? MAs disagree on whether low or high dose works better.
- Is the preclinical tumour-promotion signal relevant to long-term human supplement use, for example in smokers?
- Clinically relevant lab artifacts at oral supplement doses (creatinine, INR) remain poorly quantified. What is the long-term regulatory path in the US after FDA enforcement discretion?
