** Identity & forms
- NAC is an acetylated cysteine derivative available as OTC oral supplement and as a regulated drug (including IV/ inhaled products depending jurisdiction) [URL:https://www.fda.gov/drugs].
** Mechanisms
- Cysteine precursor for glutathione synthesis; reduces disulfide bonds in mucus; pharmacologic antidote replenishes glutathione in acetaminophen poisoning [PMID:30855987].

** Dosing in trials
- Oral COPD studies typically 600–1200 mg/day; psychiatric trials often 1.2–3 g/day, heterogeneous. Overdose treatment uses protocol-based IV/oral medical dosing [PMID:42473447].
** Safety & adverse effects
- Oral GI upset; inhaled bronchospasm; IV anaphylactoid reactions. Do not delay emergency overdose treatment.
** Contraindications
- Caution asthma/bronchospasm and active peptic symptoms; overdose is emergency treatment, not self-care.
** Interactions
- May potentiate nitroglycerin vasodilation/headache; caution with anticoagulants based on limited evidence. Activated charcoal may reduce oral antidote absorption.

** Latest research
- 2026-07: 2026 COPD meta-analysis (14 RCTs, n=2,856) reported fewer exacerbations RR 0.87 (0.79–0.96), without significant FEV1/QOL improvement; study supports cautious interpretation [PMID:42473447].
