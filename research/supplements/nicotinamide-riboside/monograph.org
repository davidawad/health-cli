** Identity & forms
- NAD+ precursor sold as chloride salt; supplement versus pharmaceutical regulation depends on jurisdiction/product. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Mechanisms
- Converted through salvage pathways to NAD+, a redox cofactor and substrate for sirtuins/PARPs; increasing blood NAD metabolites is a surrogate, not proof of clinical benefit. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]

** Dosing in trials
- Common RCT doses 250-1000 mg/day; phase I Parkinson disease trial studied 3000 mg/day for 4 weeks [PMID:38016950]. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Short-term trials report mostly mild events (GI symptoms, headache, fatigue); long-term safety and effects on disease outcomes are uncertain. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Contraindications
- No proven disease-specific contraindication; insufficient evidence for pregnancy/lactation and prolonged use. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Interactions
- Potential interaction concerns with glucose-lowering or cancer therapies are theoretical and inadequately studied; check product stacking with niacin/NAM. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Genotype notes
- No validated genotype-guided NR response evidence identified.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: 2024 systematic review assessed human NAD precursor RCTs and concluded the evidence base remains limited [PMID:37971292]. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
** Open questions
- Long-term safety, tissue-specific NAD effects, and patient-important outcomes are open questions. [PMID:37971292] [PMID:38016950]
