** Identity & forms
- NR is a pyridine-nucleoside form of vitamin B3. It occurs naturally in milk and is phosphorylated by the NRK1/NRK2 kinases, a route to NAD+ that does not depend on Preiss-Handler [PMID:15137942].
- Commercial forms: NR chloride (NIAGEN) is GRAS for foods, with two New Dietary Ingredient notifications [PMID:31278280]. NR-E is another synthetic high-purity NR [PMID:32715855]. Combination products include NRPT (NR + pterostilbene; Basis/EH301) [PMID:36082508] [PMID:30668199], and "combined metabolic activators" (CMA: NR + N-acetylcysteine + L-serine + L-carnitine) [PMID:34180141].
- NR, NMN, nicotinamide (NAM) and nicotinic acid (NA) all feed the NAD+ metabolome. NR and NMN behave differently depending on tissue enzymes and nucleotidases [PMID:29249689].

** Mechanisms
- Target engagement (NAD metabolome): oral NR raises blood NAD+ dose-dependently. Nicotinic acid adenine dinucleotide (NAAD) is a sensitive biomarker of NR intake [PMID:27721479] [PMID:31278280]. Blood NAD+ plateaus after about 2 weeks, brain NAD rises measurably by 4 weeks, and once-daily dosing is enough [PMID:41858901].
- Brain: 31P-MRS and downfield 1H-MRS detect higher cerebral NAD after NR [PMID:35235774] [PMID:39044608]. NAD+ also rises in neuron-enriched plasma extracellular vesicles [PMID:36515353].
- Gut microbiome route: In mice, most oral NR and NMN is deamidated by gut microbes to NA and enters the liver via enterohepatic circulation [PMID:36476934] [PMID:40117359]. In a 65-person human RCT, NR and NMN (but not NAM) raised circulating NAD+ to a similar degree. Ex vivo they generated NA, which supports a Preiss-Handler mechanism [PMID:41540253]. Nicotinic acid riboside (NaR) is an endogenous liver-to-kidney NAD carrier [PMID:40315855].
- Effects downstream of NAD: NAD is a substrate for sirtuins, PARPs and CD38. Compartmentalization and non-canonical breakdown routes are still poorly understood [PMID:39026037]. Excess NAM is cleared by SAM-dependent methylation to N1-methylnicotinamide and then to 2PY/4PY [PMID:21918528] [PMID:38374343].
- Anti-inflammatory signatures: Lower circulating cytokines in aged men [PMID:31412242] and in Parkinson's disease (PD) serum and CSF [PMID:35235774]. Lower sputum IL-8 in COPD [PMID:39548320]. Blunted Th17 responses through Nrf2/arginine [PMID:37586364] and SLIT2-ROBO1 signaling [PMID:42048163]. Lower NLRP3 expression in heart failure PBMCs [PMID:36644285] [PMID:32790648].
- Muscle: in obese men, NR lowered muscle NAMPT protein by 14% with no change in muscle NAD+ or mitochondrial respiration [PMID:31710095]. In BMI-discordant twins, 5 months of NR increased muscle mitochondrial number and changed DNA methylation [PMID:36638183]. Muscle epigenetic age acceleration fell with NR [PMID:42481916].
- Blood NAD+ as a biomarker: whole-blood NAD+ does not vary with age or lifestyle interventions but rises with NR. Blood NAD+ therefore shows exposure, not "biological age" [PMID:42135539].

** Dosing in trials
- Range: 100-3000 mg/d. 1000 mg/d (often 500 mg twice daily) is most common [PMID:31278280] [PMID:29599478] [PMID:42848371].
- Longest high-quality exposure: 500 mg twice daily for 52 weeks (NOPARK) [PMID:42848371]. In Werner syndrome, 1000 mg/d was given for 26 weeks (crossover) [PMID:40459998].
- Higher doses: 2000 mg/d in obese men (12 wk) [PMID:29992272], HFrEF [PMID:36644285] and long COVID [PMID:41357333]. 3000 mg/d for 4 weeks in PD (NR-SAFE) [PMID:38016950].
- Weight-based 300-900 mg/d in Friedreich's ataxia (ages 10-40) [PMID:42009009]. A 1000 mg/d escalating schedule was used in MCI [PMID:37994989].
- NRPT: 250 mg NR + 50 mg pterostilbene is the standard daily dose, with doses up to 1000/200 mg twice daily in AKI [PMID:32791973].
- Blood NAD needs about 2 weeks to reach steady state [PMID:41858901].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Tolerability: In RCTs up to 2-3 g/d for 4-52 weeks, adverse events were no more frequent than with placebo [PMID:31278280] [PMID:29992272] [PMID:38016950]. In NOPARK, serious AEs occurred in 8.3% on NR vs 14.1% on placebo [PMID:42848371]. In long COVID, dropout was higher in the NR arm (32.4% vs 14.3% at 10 weeks) [PMID:41357333].
- Flushing: no flushing at up to 1000 mg/d [PMID:31278280]. In contrast, nicotinic acid flushes through GPR109A/HCA2-PGD2 [PMID:39322100] and caused flushing plus higher bilirubin in a CMA comparison [PMID:37271226].
- Clinical worsening in PD: NOPARK found worse MDS-UPDRS and non-motor scores with NR [PMID:42848371].
- Methylation and homocysteine: NAM clearance uses methyl groups. NR-SAFE found a slight early homocysteine rise, with the methyl donor pool intact [PMID:38016950]. No change in DNA methylation, including in MTHFR variant carriers [PMID:36936793]. NIAGEN did not disturb 1-carbon metabolism [PMID:31278280]. An acute nicotinamide load lowered plasma betaine and raised homocysteine [PMID:21918528].
- Platelets: NR raised platelet NAD about 10-fold ex vivo without changing platelet function or survival [PMID:31652008]. Its catabolite NAM caused thrombocytopenia in hemodialysis patients [PMID:21270914]. No platelet signal has been reported in NR RCTs, so this is a class concern only.
- Cardiovascular catabolites: The terminal niacin metabolites 2PY/4PY predicted 3-year MACE (4PY HR 1.89-1.99) and promote VCAM-1 vascular inflammation [PMID:38374343]. High-dose NR raised 4PY and enlarged atherosclerotic plaques in Apoe-/- mice [PMID:40215896]. NR raised NAD catabolites in hypertensive adults [PMID:40770531].
- Cancer (preclinical): NR increased cancer prevalence and brain metastasis in a triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) mouse model [PMID:36371959]. NAM/NMN conferred chemoresistance in pancreatic cancer models [PMID:41724424]. Contrary data: NR potentiated gemcitabine [PMID:41349909] and paclitaxel [PMID:32433266] and did not blunt cisplatin [PMID:35875690].
- Toxicology: NIAGEN was not genotoxic. In 90-day rat studies the LOAEL was 1000 and the NOAEL 300 mg/kg/d, with a target-organ profile (liver, kidney, ovaries, testes) similar to NAM [PMID:26791540]. For NR-E the NOAEL was 500 (male) / 1200 (female) mg/kg/d [PMID:32715855].

** Contraindications
- Parkinson's disease: avoid for disease modification. The phase 3 trial showed worse outcomes than placebo [PMID:42848371].
- Active cancer, especially during chemotherapy, or a history of TNBC: caution because of preclinical tumor-promotion and chemoresistance data [PMID:36371959] [PMID:41724424].
- Pregnancy/lactation: no human safety data. Maternal NR benefited offspring in mice [PMID:30673618]. A lactation trial is recruiting [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04614714].
- Dialysis/advanced CKD: the NAM catabolite load is relevant to platelets [PMID:21270914]. An NR CKD trial showed no change in eGFR [PMID:37159264].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Chemotherapy (gemcitabine, platinum, taxanes, 5-FU): preclinical data conflict. NAD precursors protected cancer cells [PMID:41724424], yet NR potentiated gemcitabine/paclitaxel [PMID:41349909] [PMID:32433266] and protected against chemotherapy-induced neuropathy [PMID:35875690] [PMID:39128506]. A human CIPN trial was terminated [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03642990].
- PARP inhibitors (olaparib): PARP consumes NAD+. Reduced NR (NRH) increased olaparib cytotoxicity in some ovarian cancer lines (in vitro only) [PMID:40004182].
- Adoptive T-cell immunotherapy: NR improved CD8+ memory and efficacy in mouse HCC [PMID:42231576].
- Metformin: combined with NR it rescued mitophagy in POLG astrocytes [PMID:34631714]. In rats, both reduced tissue aging [PMID:39872949]. No human interaction study exists.
- Antihypertensives: post hoc nighttime BP fell only in participants not taking antihypertensives [PMID:40770531]. The precursor-class BP effect is small [PMID:37593976].
- Nicotinic acid (Rx niacin): additive B3 load. Niacin, unlike NR, causes flushing and raises glucose/HbA1c [PMID:37271226] [PMID:38915015].
*** Other supplements
- NMN: same pathway with comparable blood NAD+ increases, so stacking is redundant and adds to the catabolite (2PY/4PY) load [PMID:41540253] [PMID:38374343]. Combination reviews: [PMID:36678315].
- Trimethylglycine (TMG): proposed methyl-donor co-supplement to offset NAM methylation [PMID:10985907] [PMID:21918528]. NR trials show only a slight homocysteine rise [PMID:38016950].
- Trans-resveratrol: in PAD, adding resveratrol to NR gave no added benefit [PMID:38871717]. The analogue pterostilbene is co-formulated in NRPT [PMID:36082508].
- B Complex: niacin/niacinamide add to total B3 and catabolite load [PMID:38374343]. Flushing comes from nicotinic acid, not NR [PMID:31278280].
- NAC: co-formulated in CMA (with NR, serine, carnitine). The CMA was positive in COVID, AD and PD cognition, but the trials are multi-ingredient [PMID:34180141] [PMID:36703196] [PMID:39816194].
- Caffeine: increases NMNAT2. With NR it partly restored NAD in Alzheimer's disease (LOAD) cells (in vitro) [PMID:35730144].
- Berberine: complementary AMPK/mitochondrial actions (review) [PMID:41516357].
- CoQ10: compared head-to-head, not combined, in CKD [PMID:37159264].
*** Conditions
- Parkinson's disease, active cancer, pregnancy and dialysis: see Contraindications.
- Ataxia telangiectasia, Werner syndrome and PAD: possible benefit from small trials [PMID:37899683] [PMID:40459998] [PMID:38871717].
- Established cardiovascular disease: 2PY/4PY-MACE association (observational) [PMID:38374343].
*** Lab-test interference
- Whole-blood NAD+ and NAAD rise with NR intake. They reflect exposure, not biological age [PMID:42135539] [PMID:27721479].
- Homocysteine may rise slightly early in use [PMID:38016950]. Creatinine fell in the Werner syndrome trial [PMID:40459998].
- No direct assay artifact was found.

** Genotype notes
- MTHFR variants: no NR-related change in DNA methylation in carriers (PD trial, n=29) [PMID:36936793].
- rs10496731 is associated with 2PY/4PY levels and sVCAM-1, so it may affect catabolite exposure (not tested with NR) [PMID:38374343].

** Latest research
- [2026-10] NOPARK phase 3 (n=410): NR 1 g/d for 52 wk worsened MDS-UPDRS by 2.72 and NMSS by 4.22 vs placebo [PMID:42848371].
- [2026-09] Fatigue NMA (27 studies): no precursor improved perceived fatigue; NMN improved endurance [PMID:42774067].
- [2026-08] NR lowered skeletal muscle epigenetic age acceleration; HIIT moved some clocks the opposite way [PMID:42481916].
- [2026-07] aMCI pilot RCT (n=42): NAD+ doubled; no cognition, CBF or BP benefit [PMID:42478598]. A-T SR: NR improved SARA in open-label studies [PMID:42387837].
- [2026-06] Blood NAD+ is stable across age and lifestyle but rises with NR [PMID:42135539]. Psoriasis RCT: NR reduced Th17 responses via SLIT2-ROBO1 [PMID:42048163].
- [2026-06] A-T transcriptomics: NR dampened interferon signatures [PMID:42449929].
- [2026-06] BREAK-AD (ketones + NR, n=30): fornix myelin preserved [PMID:42344884].
- [2026-05] Friedreich's ataxia RCT: NR + exercise raised peak VO2 vs control but not vs exercise alone [PMID:42009009].
- [2026-05] NAM/NMN promoted pancreatic cancer chemoresistance in mice [PMID:41724424].
- [2026-04] SR (33 human studies): consistent target engagement; clinical effects inconclusive [PMID:41655607].
- [2026-03] NAD-brain PK: NAD plateaus at about 2 wk; brain NAD up at 4 wk [PMID:41858901].
- [2026-03] Phase 2 nerve RCT: no prevention of capsaicin axon degeneration [PMID:41502156].
- [2026-02] NR potentiated gemcitabine in PDAC models [PMID:41349909].
- [2026-01] NR vs NMN vs NAM RCT: NR ~ NMN, gut conversion to NA [PMID:41540253].
- [2025-12] NR + exercise in hypertension: no SBP benefit over exercise [PMID:40770531].
- [2025-11] Long-COVID RCT (2 g/d): NAD+ up 2.6-3.1x; no cognitive benefit [PMID:41357333].
- [2025-08] Werner syndrome RCT: CAVI improved and ulcers shrank [PMID:40459998].
- [2025-06] SR/MA: NR/NMN do not preserve muscle mass/function [PMID:40275690].
- [2025-05] High-dose NR worsened atherosclerosis in Apoe-/- mice [PMID:40215896].
- [2025-03] Enterohepatic/microbial conversion of NR to NA [PMID:40117359]. CKD: NR lowered oxidative stress [PMID:39847432].
- [2025-01] SCD/MCI crossover RCT: pTau217 fell; no RBANS effect [PMID:39817194].
- [2024-12] COPD RCT: sputum IL-8 -52.6% [PMID:39548320].

** Open questions
- Why NOPARK showed harm after positive phase 1 signals, and whether the harm generalizes to other neurodegenerative uses [PMID:42848371].
- Whether NR at supplement doses promotes tumor progression or metastasis in humans [PMID:36371959] [PMID:40926126].
- Long-term cardiovascular effect of chronically raised 2PY/4PY [PMID:38374343].
- Whether tissue NAD (not blood) increases in target organs, and which pathway (direct vs microbial NA) dominates [PMID:41540253] [PMID:39026037].
- Confirmation of PAD, A-T and COPD signals in larger trials (NICE-PAD II planned) [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07782671].
