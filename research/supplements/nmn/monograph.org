** Identity & forms
- β-nicotinamide mononucleotide is a nucleotide intermediate of NAD+ biosynthesis. NAMPT makes it from nicotinamide, and NMNAT enzymes convert it to NAD+ [PMID:29249689] [PMID:37364580].
- Forms used in trials: generic or brand β-NMN powder or capsules at 125-1250 mg/d, mostly from Japanese and Chinese manufacturers [PMID:35479740] [PMID:36002548] [PMID:36482258]. MIB-626 (Metro International Biotech) is a pharmaceutical-grade microcrystalline β-NMN polymorph studied under IND at 1000-2000 mg/d [PMID:35182418] [PMID:36740954].
- Branded high-purity ingredients with toxicology dossiers include NMN-C [PMID:33587977] and Restorin [PMID:34867355]. Intravenous [PMID:36225528] and sublingual NMN [PMID:42304075] have been tested in small human studies.
- Product quality is poor and variable. Tested commercial NMN and urolithin A products deviated from label claims by +28.6% to -100% [PMID:38935229]. An isomer-specific LC-MS/MS method found α/β-isomer mixtures and undeclared or mislabeled ingredients in 8 commercial NMN products [PMID:41545110].
- Regulatory status, by jurisdiction:
  - US, 2022: in late 2022 FDA told NMN ingredient notifiers that NMN was excluded from the dietary-supplement definition under the drug-preclusion ("race-to-market") clause. The reason given was that NMN had been authorized for investigation as a new drug (MIB-626) [URL:https://www.supplysidesj.com/regulatory/anh-npa-ask-fda-reverse-position-nmn-supplements]. FDA also retroactively rejected a previously acknowledged NDI notification [URL:https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/fda-responds-to-citizen-petitions-on-nmn-amending-its-position-on-the-ingredient-s-status-as-a-dietary-ingredient]. NPA and ANH-USA filed a citizen petition on 7 March 2023. NPA sued FDA in August 2024. The case was then stayed, and FDA exercised enforcement discretion in the meantime [URL:https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/12/09/fda-reinstates-ndi-status-of-nmn-with-new-letters-to-ingredient-players].
  - US, 2025 reversal: on 29 September 2025 FDA granted the petitions in part. It accepted evidence that NMN was marketed as a supplement in the US from 2017, before any IND authorization, so NMN is not excluded [URL:https://www.npanational.org/news/amid-pressure-from-npa-fda-declares-nmn-lawful-in-dietary-supplements/]. FDA did not disclose the IND date or define "substantial clinical investigation" [URL:https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/fda-responds-to-citizen-petitions-on-nmn-amending-its-position-on-the-ingredient-s-status-as-a-dietary-ingredient]. On 2 December 2025 FDA sent letters reinstating the NDI notifications for SyncoZymes (NDIN 1247) and Kingdomway (NDIN 1259) [URL:https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/12/09/fda-reinstates-ndi-status-of-nmn-with-new-letters-to-ingredient-players].
  - EU: NMN is an unauthorised novel food. In May 2026 EFSA's NDA Panel issued a positive opinion on one synthetic β-NMN at up to 300 mg/d in food supplements, for adults excluding pregnant and lactating women. Commission authorisation was still pending at the research date [PMID:42125559].
- RX_STATUS rationale: otc. Since the September 2025 FDA reversal, β-NMN is lawfully marketable as a US dietary supplement [URL:https://www.npanational.org/news/amid-pressure-from-npa-fda-declares-nmn-lawful-in-dietary-supplements/]. "research-chemical" would only describe the 2022-2025 interim and the EU pre-authorisation status.

** Mechanisms
- Raises the NAD+ pool, the cofactor for sirtuins, PARPs and CD38. NAD+ falls with age in mouse tissues, and NMN given to mice for 12 months reduced age-related weight gain and improved insulin sensitivity, lipids, activity and eye function [PMID:28068222] [PMID:29249689].
- Absorption route is disputed. Slc12a8 was proposed as an intestinal NMN transporter [PMID:31131364], but this was contested for lack of evidence [PMID:32694648]. In mice, most oral NMN and NR is deamidated by gut microbes to nicotinic acid and enters NAD+ synthesis by the Preiss-Handler pathway and enterohepatic recycling [PMID:40117359].
- A 2026 human RCT (n=65) found that 14 days of NR and NMN raised blood NAD+ comparably, whereas nicotinamide did not; ex vivo, microbiota converted NR and NMN to nicotinic acid [PMID:41540253]. CD38 can base-exchange NMN to nicotinic acid mononucleotide (NaMN) [PMID:39894219], consistent with human trials showing rises in NaMN rather than NMN in blood [PMID:35479740].
- CD38 on senescence-associated immune cells degrades extracellular NMN and NAD+ with age [PMID:33199925]. A 2026 phase 1/2 study builds on this CD38-NAD+-macrophage axis in immune thrombocytopenia [PMID:42056497].
- Catabolism runs through nicotinamide to methylnicotinamide and the pyridones 2PY/4PY. NNMT uses SAM for this, so heavy nicotinamide flux consumes methyl groups [PMID:31685720] [PMID:30226369] [PMID:36897780].
- Response varies widely between people. Within-group change in blood NAD had a coefficient of variation of 29-113% [PMID:38430946]. Microbiome, genetics and physiological state likely contribute [PMID:38921475].

** Dosing in trials
- Typical oral dosing in RCTs is 250-2000 mg/d for 14 days to 24 weeks [PMID:42514320] [PMID:39531138]. Japanese trials in older adults and healthy volunteers used 250 mg/d for 12 weeks [PMID:35479740] [PMID:35927255] [PMID:38789831], and 24 weeks in older men with diabetes [PMID:36443648]. An open-label 8-week study in middle-aged men (250 mg/d) raised PBMC NAD+ [PMID:38191197].
- Dose-finding RCT in 80 middle-aged adults (300/600/900 mg for 60 days): blood NAD and 6MWT gains plateaued at 600 mg/d [PMID:36482258]. A post hoc analysis estimated the median effective NAD rise for 6MWT benefit at 15.7 nmol/L [PMID:38430946].
- Highest doses tested: 1250 mg/d once daily for 4 weeks was safe in healthy adults [PMID:36002548]. MIB-626 1000 mg twice daily for 14-28 days was tested in older overweight adults and in COVID-19 with AKI [PMID:35182418] [PMID:36740954] [PMID:40746868].
- A 24-week phase 2a trial of MIB-626 1000 mg twice daily in diabetic kidney disease (n=156) is under way [PMID:42603990].
- Timing: afternoon intake gave the largest effects on lower-limb function and drowsiness in one RCT [PMID:35215405].
- Other routes: sublingual NMN raised 2PY/4PY faster than oral but did not change blood NMN, NAM or NAD+ over 60 minutes [PMID:42304075]. IV NMN was safe in 10 volunteers [PMID:36225528].
- EU proposed maximum is 300 mg/d. This equals 109.7 mg/d nicotinamide (conversion factor 1): about 5 times the niacin PRI but below the 900 mg/d nicotinamide UL [PMID:42125559].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Human RCT data show no excess adverse events. A 2026 SR/MA (15 RCTs, 10 with safety data; 250-2000 mg/d; up to 24 weeks): no increase in overall, serious, withdrawal-related or system-specific AEs, and no ALT/AST rise [PMID:42514320]. An earlier SR of 10 RCTs (n=437) found no serious AEs attributable to NMN [PMID:39221308].
- Single 100-500 mg doses in healthy men caused no clinical changes; lab values shifted within normal range (bilirubin up; creatinine, chloride and glucose down) [PMID:31685720]. 1250 mg/d for 4 weeks caused no changes beyond physiological variation [PMID:36002548].
- Long-term safety beyond 24 weeks in humans is unknown. Systematic reviews consistently call for longer, larger trials [PMID:41655607] [PMID:40926126] [PMID:37619764].
- Animal toxicology: NMN-C was not genotoxic, with a rat 90-day NOAEL >=1500 mg/kg/d [PMID:33587977]. Restorin had a rat NOAEL of 500 mg/kg/d in males and 1000 mg/kg/d in females, with reduced weight gain at 2000 mg/kg/d [PMID:34867355].
- Subacute high doses caused a mild ALT rise in mice and creatinine/uric acid rises in dogs [PMID:33384603]. The EFSA dossier's rat NOAEL was 400 mg/kg/d based on reproductive-organ effects, a margin of exposure of 93 [PMID:42125559].
- Preclinical signals of concern: NMN raised uremic toxins and inflammatory/tubule-injury markers in aged but not young mouse kidneys (preprint) [PMID:38645109]. NMN and NR worsened atherosclerosis in ApoE-/- mice at about 300 mg/kg, even while lowering lipids [PMID:40143060].
- NMN blunted the BFR-exercise-induced rise in muscle mitochondrial content in a small human crossover trial (n=11) [PMID:41705654].
- Nicotinamide load: high-dose nicotinamide may inhibit PARPs and alter methyl metabolism and epigenetics. Current usage levels are judged safe [PMID:32365524]. In a hemodialysis RCT, nicotinamide 500-1000 mg/d caused thrombocytopenia [PMID:21270914] [PMID:33229753]. This is not observed with NMN, which instead raised platelets in ITP [PMID:42056497].
- Cancer data are only preclinical and conflicting (see Contraindications and Interactions). NMN fostered chemoresistance in pancreatic cancer [PMID:41724424], but suppressed some tumours or protected against chemotherapy toxicity [PMID:39169162] [PMID:37173894] [PMID:36611902]. Lifelong NMN in mice did not raise tumour burden (preprint) [PMID:38979132].

** Contraindications
- Pregnancy and lactation: no human data. EFSA excluded these groups from its safety conclusion [PMID:42125559].
- Active cancer, especially during chemotherapy: NAD+ supports tumour metabolism, and NAMPT/NAPRT-mediated NAD synthesis drives resistance [PMID:38396769] [PMID:39661983]. NMN conferred resistance to oxaliplatin, 5-FU and gemcitabine and supported PDAC growth in mice [PMID:41724424]. The direction of effect differs by tumour [PMID:37175631] [PMID:28780936].
- Children: no trials. The EU opinion covers adults only [PMID:42125559].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Cytotoxic chemotherapy: preclinical data conflict. NMN increased resistance of PDAC cells and mouse tumours to oxaliplatin, 5-FU and gemcitabine [PMID:41724424].
- In a TNBC mouse model, NMN did not impair chemotherapy efficacy and protected fertility [PMID:39169162]. It also protected ovaries against cyclophosphamide [PMID:40849491] and against doxorubicin cardiotoxicity and fibrosis [PMID:36611902] [PMID:38791345]. No human interaction data exist, so oncologist oversight is warranted.
- NAD-depleting anticancer agents (NAMPT inhibitors, NAD-restriction strategies with melphalan): exogenous precursors bypass NAMPT blockade and would be expected to antagonize them [PMID:38396769] [PMID:39661983].
- Metformin: metformin raises the NADH/NAD+ ratio. In cancer cells, NMN reversed metformin's NAD-depletion-dependent effects (synthetic lethality with MCT1/4 inhibition; suppressed migration) [PMID:30540938] [PMID:42414524]. Clinical significance for glucose lowering is unknown. NMN has no pooled glycaemic effect [PMID:39531138].
- Antihypertensives: modest additive DBP reduction is possible (WMD -2.15 mmHg) [PMID:41901064] [PMID:36740954].
- Cell therapy (CAR-NK): NMN preconditioning enhanced MSLN CAR-NK activity in an ovarian cancer xenograft. This is preclinical and not a supplement-use interaction [PMID:42595793].
*** Other supplements
- Nicotinamide Riboside: same pathway. In a head-to-head RCT, NR and NMN raised blood NAD+ comparably, both largely via microbial conversion to nicotinic acid [PMID:41540253] [PMID:40117359]. Stacking adds nicotinamide/methyl-group load without evidence of additive benefit [PMID:32365524].
- Trimethylglycine (TMG): nicotinamide clearance by NNMT consumes SAM and lowered hepatic betaine and DNA methylation in rats [PMID:23768418] [PMID:2969042] [PMID:30226369]. This underlies the common practice of pairing NAD precursors with a methyl donor. Human NR data show only a transient homocysteine rise with an intact methyl pool, and no change in DNA methylation [PMID:38016950] [PMID:36936793] [PMID:31278280]. No NMN+TMG trial exists.
- Trans-resveratrol: theoretical synergy through the NAD+/SIRT1 axis [PMID:36678315]. The analogue stilbene pterostilbene combined with NR safely raised NAD+ in RCTs [PMID:29184669] [PMID:32791973] [PMID:42211736]. No NMN+resveratrol human trial was found.
- B Complex / niacinamide-containing products: they add to nicotinamide intake. 300 mg NMN is about 110 mg nicotinamide equivalent, against a nicotinamide UL of 900 mg/d [PMID:42125559].
- PQQ: combining PQQ with NMN gave no added interoception benefit over PQQ alone in one RCT [PMID:41651893].
*** Conditions
- Active cancer, pregnancy and lactation: see Contraindications.
- Advanced age with kidney dysfunction: aged-mouse data show uremic-toxin accumulation and kidney inflammation [PMID:38645109]. In COVID-19 with AKI, 2 g/d MIB-626 for 14 days was safe but did not improve kidney markers [PMID:40746868].
- Atherosclerotic disease: mouse ApoE-/- signal only [PMID:40143060].
- Hemodialysis: nicotinamide-induced thrombocytopenia has been seen with direct nicotinamide [PMID:21270914] [PMID:33229753]. This is an extrapolation to NMN.
- Exercise training: NMN with endurance training improved ventilatory thresholds [PMID:34238308]. However, it blunted acute mitochondrial adaptation after BFR resistance exercise [PMID:41705654].
*** Lab-test interference
- Niacin status markers: NMN dose-dependently raises plasma and urinary 2PY/4PY and methylnicotinamide [PMID:31685720] [PMID:42304075] [PMID:40746868]. These markers will reflect supplement intake rather than diet.
- Within-normal-range shifts after NMN: bilirubin up, creatinine down [PMID:31685720]. NMN-induced NAD rises were associated with higher haemoglobin, RBC count and uric acid in a post hoc analysis [PMID:41162813].
- Whole-blood NAD assays vary widely between labs and people, which complicates dose titration [PMID:38430946].

** Genotype notes
- A blinded NR trial found no DNA-methylation disturbance, including in carriers of common MTHFR variants [PMID:36936793]. No NMN-specific pharmacogenomic study was found.

** Latest research
- [2026-10] NOPARK phase 3 (NR 1 g/d for 52 weeks, n=410, Parkinson disease): MDS-UPDRS worsened 2.72 points vs placebo. This is a class signal that NAD precursors can fail or harm in long, large trials [PMID:42848371].
- [2026-09] Network MA (27 studies, n=1599): NMN improves endurance (SMD 0.66-1.02), not strength, fatigue or sleep [PMID:42774067].
- [2026-07] SR/MA (15 RCTs): NMN is safe short-term; no broad metabolic benefit; DBP slightly lower [PMID:42514320].
- [2026-07] Phase 2a DKD trial protocol (MIB-626 2 g/d for 24 weeks, n=156) published [PMID:42603990].
- [2026-06] Sublingual vs oral crossover PK (n=14): sublingual raises 2PY/4PY faster, with no NAD+ difference [PMID:42304075].
- [2026-05] EFSA positive novel-food opinion: β-NMN up to 300 mg/d for adults [PMID:42125559].
- [2026-05] Retinal thickness RCT in older diabetics: safe; small temporal-region difference [PMID:42082179].
- [2026-05] NR plus exercise RCT in Friedreich ataxia: combination raised peak VO2 vs control but not vs exercise alone [PMID:42009009].
- [2026-04] Phase 1/2 oral NMN 900 mg/d in refractory ITP (n=25): well tolerated; 20% primary responders [PMID:42056497].
- [2026-03] BP MA (10 RCTs): DBP -2.15 mmHg [PMID:41901064].
- [2026-02] PDAC study: NMN promotes chemoresistance in vitro and in mice [PMID:41724424].
- [2026-02] Human BFR-exercise crossover: NMN suppressed TNF-α but abolished the mitochondrial-content rise [PMID:41705654].
- [2026-02] PRISMA SR (33 human studies): target engagement consistent; clinical outcomes heterogeneous or null [PMID:41655607].
- [2026-01] Human RCT (n=65): NR = NMN > nicotinamide for NAD+ rise; gut conversion to nicotinic acid [PMID:41540253].
- [2025-12] FDA reinstated NMN NDI notifications on 2 Dec 2025 [URL:https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/12/09/fda-reinstates-ndi-status-of-nmn-with-new-letters-to-ingredient-players].
- [2025-10] Post hoc analysis: NMN-induced NAD rise tracks RBC and haemoglobin increases [PMID:41162813].
- [2025-09] FDA reversed its 2022 drug-preclusion position on NMN [URL:https://www.npanational.org/news/amid-pressure-from-npa-fda-declares-nmn-lawful-in-dietary-supplements/].
- [2025-06] Sarcopenia MA: NMN/NR do not improve muscle mass, grip or gait in adults over 60 [PMID:40275690].
- [2025-06] MIB-626 RCT in COVID-19 with AKI: NAD+ rose about 2.7-fold; no effect on AKI or inflammation [PMID:40746868].

** Open questions
- Whether consistent NAD+ elevation translates into clinical benefit. Most functional and metabolic endpoints are null or small, and many positive trials are industry-funded with high or unclear risk of bias [PMID:39116016] [PMID:41655607].
- Long-term (>6 month) safety, particularly regarding cancer progression in people with undiagnosed or active tumours, kidney function in older adults, and atherosclerosis [PMID:41724424] [PMID:38645109] [PMID:40143060].
- The true absorption route (Slc12a8 vs microbial deamidation), and whether NMN offers anything beyond cheaper nicotinic acid or NR [PMID:32694648] [PMID:41540253].
- Whether NAD precursors blunt training adaptations [PMID:41705654].
- Product authenticity and α/β-isomer purity of retail products [PMID:38935229] [PMID:41545110].
- Results from the MIB-626 DKD trial and any EU authorisation decision [PMID:42603990] [PMID:42125559].
