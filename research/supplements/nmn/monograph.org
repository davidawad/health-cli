** Identity & forms
- Nicotinamide mononucleotide, an NAD+ precursor sold as a dietary supplement in some jurisdictions; regulatory status differs. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Mechanisms
- Converted to NAD+ through salvage metabolism; NAD change does not imply improved healthspan. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]

** Dosing in trials
- Human RCTs generally use 250-1200 mg/day for 4-12 weeks; no established therapeutic dose. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Short trials generally report tolerability, but sample sizes/duration cannot establish chronic safety. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Contraindications
- No established medical contraindications; insufficient pregnancy, lactation, cancer-survivor and long-term safety data. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Interactions
- Drug interactions are not adequately characterized; theoretical overlap with glucose-lowering/cancer therapies. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Genotype notes
- No validated genotype-guided selection evidence identified.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: 2025 meta-analysis found no significant improvement in several skeletal muscle function outcomes with NMN/NR [PMID:40275690]. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
** Open questions
- Whether biomarker elevation leads to durable clinical benefit or alters disease risk is unknown. [PMID:39116016] [PMID:40275690] [PMID:39221308]
