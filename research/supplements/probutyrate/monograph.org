** Identity & forms
- ProButyrate is a branded sustained-release butyric acid product. The manufacturer lists 600 mg natural butyric acid proprietary complex per 2 capsules, using a dextrin carrier and release matrix; it is not equivalent to sodium butyrate or dietary fiber [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Mechanisms
- Butyrate is a colonocyte energy substrate and can signal through short-chain-fatty-acid receptors and histone deacetylase pathways. Delivery to the colon and clinical effects of this specific formulation have not been demonstrated in independent human trials [DOI:10.1038/nrgastro.2017.51; URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Dosing in trials
- Manufacturer label: 2 capsules provide 600 mg butyric acid complex; suggested use is 1–3 capsules twice daily. This is product-label information, not an evidence-based therapeutic dose [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Safety & adverse effects
- No published large safety trial for this exact sustained-release product was located. Oral butyrate products may cause GI discomfort; excipients include cellulose-derived capsule/release agents and calcium laurate according to manufacturer [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Contraindications
- Avoid if allergic to product ingredients. Product efficacy and long-term safety in pregnancy, lactation, significant GI disease or children are unestablished; no formal product-specific contraindication studies identified [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Interactions
- No clinically established drug interactions or laboratory assay interference identified for the exact product. The manufacturer identifies calcium laurate as an excipient; no evidence it provides a clinically meaningful calcium interaction [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source specific to ProButyrate was identified in this search; no genotype claim is made.

** Latest research
- Current manufacturer page specifies a sustained-release, free butyric acid/dextrin complex and 600 mg per 2-capsule serving; independent product-level human efficacy evidence was not located in PubMed searches through 2026-10-13 [URL:https://drhyman.com/products/probutyrate].

** Open questions
- Larger, better reported human studies with standardized products, clinically meaningful endpoints and longer follow-up are needed; current gaps are noted above.
