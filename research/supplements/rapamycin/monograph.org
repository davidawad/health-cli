** Identity & forms
- Sirolimus (rapamycin) is a macrocyclic lactone from Streptomyces hygroscopicus. It is a prescription mTOR-inhibiting immunosuppressant [PMID:15691225] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85].
- Approved uses: Oral tablets/solution: renal transplant rejection prophylaxis (age >=13) and lymphangioleiomyomatosis (LAM) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]. IV nab-sirolimus: approved in the US for malignant PEComa, based on AMPECT [PMID:38427923] [PMID:34637337]. Topical sirolimus (mostly 0.1%): RCT evidence for facial angiofibromas in tuberous sclerosis (799 patients) [PMID:39018447].
- Longevity use is off-label, usually intermittent low doses (e.g. 5-10 mg weekly), often compounded [PMID:40188830] [PMID:37191826]. Rapalogs include everolimus (RAD001) and temsirolimus. RTB101 is a catalytic TORC1 inhibitor [PMID:33977284] [PMID:29997249].
- Pharmacokinetics: tmax 1-2 h, oral bioavailability ~14%, blood/plasma ratio ~36 [PMID:10823378]. Half-life ~62 h, with clearance CV 52% between and 26% within people [PMID:10823378]. Weekly dosing in Alzheimer's disease gave a half-life of ~69 h [PMID:41046300]. Metabolized by CYP3A4/5; a P-gp substrate [PMID:11523724] [PMID:27128230].

** Mechanisms
- Rapamycin-FKBP12 allosterically inhibits mTORC1, arresting T- and B-cell cycling [PMID:15691225] [PMID:37142830].
- Chronic exposure also disrupts mTORC2. In mice this drives hepatic insulin resistance, which is separable from the longevity effect [PMID:22461615].
- Intermittent dosing or rapalogs spare glucose tolerance, beta-cell function and immune cells in mice [PMID:26463117].
- Rapamycin and acarbose share mTORC1 suppression and increased cap-independent translation [PMID:33742521].
- Immune aging: Low-dose TORC1 inhibition raised influenza vaccine titers, lowered PD-1+ T cells and upregulated interferon antiviral genes [PMID:25540326] [PMID:29997249] [PMID:33977284]. Sustained T-cell mTORC1 activity impairs vaccine responses in older people [PMID:40249803].
- Mouse tissue effects: Reversed periodontal bone loss and inflammation and made the oral microbiome more youthful [PMID:28889220] [PMID:32342860]. Preserved primordial ovarian follicles, with FOXO3a upregulation [PMID:31359237] [PMID:28544226].
- Human skin: topical use lowered p16INK4A and raised collagen VII [PMID:31761958].

** Dosing in trials
- Label regimens [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]: Renal transplant after cyclosporine withdrawal: trough 16-24 ng/mL in year 1, then 12-20 ng/mL. Take 4 h after cyclosporine. LAM: trough 5-15 ng/mL. MILES treated for 12 months [PMID:21410393].
- PEComa: nab-sirolimus 100 mg/m2 IV on days 1 and 8 every 21 days [PMID:34637337].
- Longevity regimens studied: PEARL: 5 or 10 mg weekly for 48 weeks [PMID:40188830]. RAPA-EX-01: 6 mg weekly for 13 weeks [PMID:41985884]. Pilot in adults 70-95: 1 mg daily for 8 weeks [PMID:29408453]. Alzheimer's disease: 1 mg daily for 8 weeks [PMID:40394335], or 7 mg weekly for 26 weeks [PMID:41046300]. VIBRANT: 5 mg weekly for 12 weeks [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05836025].
- Rapalog immune trials: low-dose RAD001 before vaccination [PMID:25540326]; BEZ235 plus RAD001 for 6 weeks [PMID:29997249]; RTB101 10 mg daily for 16 weeks [PMID:33977284].
- Dogs: 0.025 mg/kg three times weekly [PMID:37275618].
- Weekly sirolimus in cancer: the dose needed for a target AUC fell from 90 mg to 16 mg with ketoconazole and to 25 mg with grapefruit juice [PMID:22872575].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Boxed warning: infection and lymphoma risk. Excess deaths and hepatic artery thrombosis in liver transplant; fatal bronchial dehiscence in lung transplant [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85].
- Stomatitis / mouth ulcers: Everolimus trials: 67-70%, grade 3/4 in 8-9%, mostly within 8 weeks [PMID:26759276]. MA high-grade RR 8.84 [PMID:25635371]. Dexamethasone mouthwash cut grade >=2 stomatitis to 2% vs 33% in historical controls [PMID:28314691]. nab-sirolimus: 82.4% [PMID:38427923]. Low-dose off-label users: ulcers mostly mild, transient and not dose-related [PMID:38839644].
- Hyperlipidemia: Label: hypertriglyceridemia 45%, hypercholesterolemia 43% [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]. Triglycerides up to 42 mmol/L, reversible on dose reduction [PMID:9603181]. MA RRs 2.22 (triglycerides) and 2.48 (cholesterol) [PMID:27069150]. Lipids also rose in people with age-related diseases on rapalogs [PMID:38310895].
- Hyperglycemia / insulin resistance: mTORi MA: all-grade RR 2.97 [PMID:27069150]. Everolimus MA: RR 2.60 [PMID:27142123]. Weekly dosing in cancer: 52% [PMID:22872575]. Sirolimus was not a risk factor for new-onset diabetes after transplant [PMID:32506801]. OGTT unchanged on 1 mg/day in older adults [PMID:29408453]; HbA1c rose in an Alzheimer's pilot [PMID:40394335]. Intermittent dosing lessens glucose intolerance in mice [PMID:26463117] [PMID:40704394].
- Wound healing / fluid: Complications 47% on sirolimus vs 8% on tacrolimus [PMID:15239621]. Lymphocele and dehiscence reviewed in [PMID:22941182]. Peripheral edema 54% at 2 mg/day (label) [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85].
- Pneumonitis: 14% after late switch vs 0% de novo. Risk factors: low GFR and trough >12 ug/L. It resolved on stopping [PMID:17611248]. MAs: any grade ~10%; severe OR 3.36 [PMID:22909392] [PMID:27067269].
- Infection: Pneumocystis pneumonia OR 1.90 [PMID:31377892]. Pneumonia OR 2.09 with mTORi monotherapy, but less CMV [PMID:32072714]. Attributable infection risk in cancer: 9.3% [PMID:27791402]. Bacterial infections 27% vs 11% when everolimus replaced mycophenolate [PMID:40231961]. One possibly drug-related pneumonia, and 99 vs 63 minor AEs, in RAPA-EX-01 [PMID:41985884].
- Cytopenias: Platelets and white cells fall at troughs >15 ng/mL [PMID:10770413]. Lymphopenia 41% on weekly dosing [PMID:22872575]. Low doses lowered hemoglobin, hematocrit and MCV in older adults [PMID:29408453] [PMID:40394335] and MCV in dogs [PMID:28374166].
- Other: Proteinuria, especially after calcineurin-inhibitor conversion [PMID:17147461]. Angioedema in 15% of renal recipients [PMID:17377381]. Kidney transplant mortality HR 1.43 [PMID:25422259].
- Low-dose healthy cohorts: AEs similar to placebo in PEARL [PMID:40188830]. No serious rapalog AEs in healthy people (SR) [PMID:38310895] [PMID:37191826].

** Contraindications
- Labeled: hypersensitivity only [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85].
- Pregnancy: fetal harm warning; contraception during use and for 12 weeks after; reversible male azoospermia. Not recommended in liver or lung transplant (same label).
- Avoid with active serious infection or recent/planned surgery [PMID:15239621] [PMID:22941182], and with interstitial lung disease or significant renal impairment [PMID:17611248].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, voriconazole, clarithromycin): avoid. Ketoconazole raised AUC ~500% [PMID:22872575] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]. Diltiazem raised AUC 60% [PMID:11180036].
- Strong inducers: rifampin cut AUC 82%; avoid [PMID:27128230].
- Cyclosporine: dose sirolimus 4 h after; sirolimus levels fall when cyclosporine is withdrawn (label). Combined nephrotoxicity, myelosuppression and hyperlipidemia worsen [PMID:11316866] [PMID:15691225].
- ACE inhibitors: higher angioedema risk; consider an ARB [PMID:38205154] [PMID:25068425] [PMID:17377381].
- Live vaccines: avoid (label). mTORi regimens kept or improved mRNA vaccine responses in transplant recipients [PMID:35038362].
- Statins: myopathy warning (label), though lipid treatment is often needed [PMID:27069150].
*** Other supplements
- Metformin ER: combination extended ITP mouse lifespan robustly vs historical rapamycin-alone cohorts [PMID:27312235]. Metformin reversed rapamycin glucose intolerance in female mice [PMID:29579736]. No human outcome data.
- Acarbose: started at 9 months, the combination gave male mice longer lifespan than rapamycin-only cohorts [PMID:36179270] [PMID:33742521].
- Berberine: raised cyclosporine AUC 19% via CYP3A4, so a sirolimus rise is plausible [PMID:16541194].
- Curcumin: lowered everolimus AUC ~71% in rats via CYP3A4 activation [PMID:25300360]. Null in the ITP [PMID:22451473].
- Green Tea Extract: no sirolimus PK study. Herbal teas inhibit CYP3A4 and caused toxic sirolimus levels in one case [PMID:24824478]. Null in the ITP [PMID:22451473].
- Quercetin: acutely inhibits and chronically induces CYP3A4/P-gp in vitro [PMID:16442130].
- Trans-resveratrol: overlapping mTOR/AMPK pharmacology [PMID:35893737]; null in the ITP [PMID:22451473]. Melatonin: no clinical interaction found.
- St John's wort: induces CYP3A4/P-gp and lowered tacrolimus AUC [PMID:31742659] [PMID:14681346]. Grapefruit juice raised sirolimus AUC ~350% [PMID:22872575].
*** Conditions
- Diabetes/prediabetes, dyslipidemia, upcoming surgery, ILD, CKD, active infection and pregnancy: see Safety and Contraindications [PMID:27069150] [PMID:17611248].
- Exercise training in older adults: 6 mg weekly may blunt gains [PMID:41985884].
*** Lab-test interference
- Immunoassay and LC-MS/MS troughs are not interchangeable (label) [PMID:11523724].
- Trough timing and targets: Trough tracks AUC; sample 4-7 days after a dose change [PMID:10823378]. <5 ng/mL is linked to rejection; >15 ng/mL to toxicity [PMID:10770413]. With weekly dosing, a 48-h post-dose sample is best [PMID:41046300].
- Monitor lipids, glucose/HbA1c, CBC, urine protein and (in LAM) VEGF-D [PMID:27069150] [PMID:29408453] [PMID:17147461] [PMID:21410393].

** Genotype notes
- CYP3A5*3/*3 non-expressers have a higher weight-adjusted trough/dose ratio (MD 95.27 ng/mL per mg/kg, 7 studies) and need lower doses [PMID:39171589].
- ABCB1 variants had no significant effect. Trough-guided dosing compensates [PMID:17192769].
- No genotype guidance exists for low-dose longevity use.

** Latest research
- [2026-06] Lamming review: rapamycin is the most reproducible geroprotector in animals, but its human value is unresolved [PMID:42126807].
- [2026-04] RAPA-EX-01 (n=40): 6 mg weekly did not improve, and may have blunted, exercise gains [PMID:41985884].
- [2026-02] RECOVAC RCT: everolimus raised zoster antibody titers but also bacterial infections [PMID:40231961].
- [2025-11] OPTIMIZE (n=379, age >=65): everolimus plus low-dose tacrolimus was not superior [PMID:41201871].
- [2025-10] Weekly 7 mg in Alzheimer's disease: half-life ~69 h; troughs below immunosuppressive levels [PMID:41046300].
- [2025-09] JAMA review: mouse median lifespan +249 d (F) and +154 d (M); rapalogs investigational in humans [PMID:40773213].
- [2025-07] Intermittent dosing preserved exercise adaptations in mice [PMID:40704394].
- [2025-06] Vertebrate MA: rapamycin, but not metformin, extends lifespan to a degree similar to dietary restriction [PMID:40532901].
- [2025-05] Alzheimer's pilot: no CSF penetration; biomarkers and HbA1c rose [PMID:40394335].
- [2025-04] PEARL: 5-10 mg weekly safe over 48 weeks; primary outcome null [PMID:40188830]. Separate study: T-cell mTORC1 impairs vaccine responses in older people [PMID:40249803].
- [2025-02] TRIAD design: randomized lifespan trial in companion dogs [PMID:39951177].
- [2024-10] Oral-health survey: mouth ulcers mostly mild [PMID:38839644]. VIBRANT ovarian pilot (n=50) is active, not recruiting, with no published results [URL:https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05836025].

** Open questions
- Whether intermittent low-dose use changes morbidity or mortality in humans. No adequately powered trial exists.
- The dose and interval that separate mTORC1 benefits from mTORC2 harms.
- Long-term effects on lipids, glucose, red-cell indices and infection in healthy users.
- Compounded vs generic bioavailability (NCT06550271).
- VIBRANT and TRIAD outcomes.
- Whether metformin or acarbose combinations add benefit in humans.
