** Identity & forms
- Sirolimus is an immunosuppressive prescription drug; longevity use in healthy people is off-label and investigational. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Mechanisms
- FKBP12 complex inhibits mTORC1; immunosuppression, altered metabolism, wound healing and cell growth effects are clinically important. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]

** Dosing in trials
- Prescription regimens for approved indications are individualized and therapeutic-drug monitored; PEARL studied 5 or 10 mg weekly for 48 weeks. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Stomatitis, infections, dyslipidemia, cytopenias, impaired wound healing, proteinuria, pneumonitis; serious immunosuppressive risks and CYP3A interactions. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Contraindications
- Contraindicated with hypersensitivity; major caution in active infection, surgery/wound healing, pregnancy, hepatic impairment, and live vaccines per labeling. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Interactions
- Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors or inducers substantially alter exposure; grapefruit may increase levels. Immunosuppressants and live vaccines pose additional risk. Consult current DailyMed label. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Genotype notes
- No validated genotype guidance for longevity use.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: 2025 PEARL report is the longest randomized healthy-aging rapamycin trial yet, but endpoints are exploratory and do not prove longevity benefit [PMID:40188830]. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
** Open questions
- Long-term benefit-risk, optimal regimen, and whether any biomarker predicts benefit are unresolved. [PMID:40188830] [PMID:40620657] [URL:https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=6a85ad01-8144-4d9c-e053-2991aa0a4f85]
