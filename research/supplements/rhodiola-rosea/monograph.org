** Identity & forms
- Rhodiola rosea L. root/rhizome extracts are standardized variably to rosavins and salidroside; extract composition differs across products [PMID:37495266].

** Mechanisms
- Proposed adaptogenic effects include modulation of stress-response and monoaminergic pathways, mainly based on preclinical and mechanistic data [PMID:22643043].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---------+--------------------+-----------+-------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| Physical fatigue/performance | systematic review of 13 RCTs, n=263 | mixed, some positive outcomes | 11 studies reported some favorable measures; heterogeneity prevents pooled effect | low | PMID:37495266 |
| Mental/physical fatigue | systematic review | inconsistent | 11 small trials, high risk of bias/reporting flaws | very low | PMID:22643043 |
| Post-COVID fatigue/cognition | 2024 narrative review | hypothesis only | no robust Rhodiola-specific estimate | very low | PMID:38347175 |

** Dosing in trials
- Trials used acute and chronic extract dosing, with formulations and doses varying substantially; review did not identify a reliable dose-response [PMID:37495266].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Trials are small and short; adverse-event and product-quality reporting are incomplete. Long-term safety is uncertain [PMID:22643043].

** Contraindications
- Insufficient evidence in pregnancy/lactation, bipolar disorder, or significant medical illness; do not interpret absence of reported harm in small trials as established safety [PMID:22643043].

** Interactions
- Drugs, supplements, conditions, and relevant laboratory interference are summarized in the structured interaction object. Potential serotonergic/activating interactions with antidepressants are mechanistically plausible but clinical evidence is limited; mention as uncertainty [PMID:22643043]. No genotype-specific claim is made because no directly relevant pharmacogenomic source was identified.

** Genotype notes
- No directly relevant, clinically validated pharmacogenomic/nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed.

** Latest research
- 2024 review discussed Rhodiola among multi-target agents proposed for post-COVID cognitive impairment/fatigue, but did not supply definitive Rhodiola-specific clinical efficacy evidence [PMID:38347175].

** Open questions
- Standardized preparations, larger independent RCTs, and clinically meaningful fatigue outcomes are needed [PMID:37495266].
