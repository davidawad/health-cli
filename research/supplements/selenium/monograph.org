** Identity & forms
- Essential trace element. It is incorporated as selenocysteine into selenoproteins: glutathione peroxidases (GPx), thioredoxin reductases, iodothyronine deiodinases and selenoprotein P (SELENOP) [PMID:22381456] [PMID:19942156].
- Supplement forms:
  - L-selenomethionine (SeMet): the main food form. It is stored in place of methionine in body proteins and released with protein turnover [PMID:10867031]. Used at 200 ug/d in SELECT [PMID:19066370].
  - Selenium-enriched yeast (Se-yeast): mostly SeMet. Used in the NPC, Denmark PRECISE and KiSel-10 trials [PMID:14519754] [PMID:29454039] [PMID:40563319]. Its kinetics resemble food selenium [PMID:18193397].
  - Sodium selenite or selenate (inorganic): used in Hashimoto trials, Keshan disease prevention, radiotherapy and IV in sepsis [PMID:23744563] [PMID:22705420] [PMID:20133068] [PMID:27428731]. Selenium nanoparticles are investigational [PMID:41825095].
- Organic forms raise blood selenium more than inorganic forms. GPx responses are similar across forms [PMID:8825978].
- Two Brazil nuts per day matched 100 ug SeMet for raising selenium status [PMID:18258628].
- Reference intakes: US RDA 55 ug/d and UL 400 ug/d [PMID:18193397]. EFSA (2023) set the adult UL at 255 ug/d, including pregnancy and lactation [PMID:36698500].

** Mechanisms
- Hepatic regulation and supply hierarchy: the liver exports SELENOP to tissues via the ApoER2 and megalin receptors. Vital selenoproteins are prioritized when selenium is scarce [PMID:25974694].
- SELENOP is both transport protein and status biomarker [PMID:36067902]. It saturates at serum selenium ~146 ug/L (GPx3 at ~99 ug/L) [PMID:38960007]; selenoproteins are thought replete at plasma ~125 ug/L [PMID:29454039].
- Thyroid: GPx scavenges H2O2 from hormone synthesis, and deiodinases DIO1-3 are selenoproteins [PMID:19942156] [PMID:41677203]. In Hashimoto thyroiditis, selenium increased activated Tregs [PMID:33650299].
- U-shaped dose-response: deficiency and excess both harm. High-dose SeMet metabolites (selenols) redox-cycle and generate superoxide; tolerance varies with SNPs, toxic-element co-exposure and form [PMID:31388899] [PMID:22381456].
- Statins could impair Sec-tRNA isopentenylation and so selenoprotein synthesis. This is a hypothesis only [PMID:15031036].
- Vitamin A suppressed hepatic SELENOP in vitro [PMID:41106613].

** Dosing in trials
- Cancer prevention: 200 ug/d as SeMet (SELECT) or Se-yeast (NPC, Sel/Cel) [PMID:19066370] [PMID:8971064] [PMID:27530657]. Denmark PRECISE used 100, 200 or 300 ug/d Se-yeast for 5 years [PMID:29454039].
- Hashimoto: usually 100-200 ug/d SeMet or selenite for 3-12 months [PMID:38243784] [PMID:27702392]. SeMet appeared more effective than selenite or Se-yeast [PMID:40898469].
- TPOAb-positive pregnancy: SeMet 200 ug/d through pregnancy and postpartum [PMID:17284630].
- Graves orbitopathy: 100 ug twice daily for 6 months [PMID:21591944]. Graves disease (GRASS): 200 ug/d for 24-30 months [PMID:41384622].
- KiSel-10: Se-yeast 200 ug/d plus CoQ10 200 mg/d for 4 years [PMID:40563319].
- Cisplatin RCT: a single 400 ug dose [PMID:24475439].
- Effect on status: SELECT raised circulating selenium by a median ~114 ug/L [PMID:29788239]. 300 ug/d Se-yeast raised plasma selenium from 142 to 228 ug/L over 48 weeks [PMID:18193397].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Selenosis outbreak: a liquid supplement with ~200x the labeled selenium (median ~41,749 ug/d) caused 201 cases: diarrhea (78%), fatigue (75%), hair loss (72%), joint pain (70%), nail discoloration or brittleness (61%). Nail and hair changes often persisted beyond 90 days [PMID:20142570].
- Upper limits: EFSA based its UL on alopecia (LOAEL 330 ug/d total intake, from SELECT). High-dose supplement users and Brazil-nut eaters may exceed it [PMID:36698500].
- SELECT (200 ug/d): more alopecia and dermatitis [PMID:29376219]; a non-significant rise in T2D (RR 1.07) [PMID:19066370].
- Type 2 diabetes: pooled RCT RR 1.11 [PMID:29974401]. Mendelian randomization gave OR 1.18 (CI 0.97-1.43) [PMID:29788239].
- Cancer: high-grade prostate cancer rose in men with high baseline status [PMID:24563519]. SCC increased in NPC [PMID:14519754]; a 2025 dose-response MA links exposure to SCC and possibly melanoma [PMID:40674897].
- All-cause mortality rose with 300 ug/d (Denmark PRECISE) [PMID:29454039].
- Thyroid trials: AEs similar to control (OR 0.89) [PMID:38243784]; an earlier MA reported slightly more AEs with selenium [PMID:27702392].
- Deficiency is harmful too: selenite programs reduced Keshan cardiomyopathy in China [PMID:22705420].

** Contraindications
- Adequate or high selenium status (e.g., most of the US). Supplementing people who are already replete may raise T2D and high-grade prostate cancer risk [PMID:22381456] [PMID:24563519] [PMID:17620655].
- Total intake above 300-400 ug/d [PMID:29454039] [PMID:18193397] [PMID:36698500].
- Combined severe iodine and selenium deficiency without correcting iodine: selenium alone may worsen hypothyroidism by increasing T4 deiodination [PMID:8427203] [PMID:12487769].
- History of non-melanoma (squamous cell) skin cancer: increased SCC in the NPC trial [PMID:14519754].
- Children: selenium-containing supplements should be used with caution [PMID:36698500].

** Interactions
*** Drugs
- Cisplatin: small RCTs suggest less nephrotoxicity and myelosuppression [PMID:24475439] [PMID:9197929]. An antioxidant-mix RCT was null [PMID:15251161], and Cochrane found insufficient evidence [PMID:16856073]. Preclinical SeMet reduced ototoxicity via GPX4 without blunting tumor killing [PMID:41698483].
- Radiotherapy and chemotherapy: benefit appears limited to selenium-deficient patients, with no OS or recurrence benefit [PMID:39374898] [PMID:20133068].
- Statins: a selenoprotein-synthesis effect is hypothesized [PMID:15031036]. CoQ10 + selenium RCTs in statin myopathy showed no selenium-specific benefit [PMID:23458201] [PMID:23301875].
- Simvastatin-niacin: an antioxidant mix including selenium blunted the HDL2 rise and the angiographic benefit (HATS) [PMID:11757504] [PMID:11498460].
- Levothyroxine: TPOAb fell in treated patients [PMID:27702392] and TSH fell in untreated patients [PMID:38243784]. Monitor TSH.
- Antithyroid drugs: selenium added no remission benefit [PMID:41384622].
*** Other supplements
- Vitamin E (SELECT): vitamin E alone raised prostate cancer risk (HR 1.17). Selenium plus vitamin E showed no benefit (HR 1.05) [PMID:21990298] [PMID:19066370]. Vitamin E harm was confined to men with low selenium status [PMID:24563519]. Neither prevented dementia [PMID:28319243].
- Iodine: correct iodine before selenium in combined deficiency [PMID:12487769] [PMID:8427203]. No synergy on thyroid function in older New Zealanders [PMID:19692495].
- Zinc: Zn/Se co-supplementation raised RMR and selenium levels but not thyroid function or zinc levels [PMID:37513551]. Zinc and selenium both act on thyroid hormone metabolism [PMID:12487769].
- CoQ10: Se + CoQ10 reduced CV mortality in low-status elderly people (KiSel-10) [PMID:26624886] [PMID:29641571] [PMID:38960007].
- Myo-inositol: added to selenium, it lowered TSH more than selenium alone in Hashimoto with subclinical hypothyroidism [PMID:42122912]. It was not superior in the network MA for antibodies [PMID:39698034].
- Antioxidant mixtures: CVD and all-cause mortality were lower only when selenium was in the mix [PMID:33053149].
- Vitamin A: suppressed SELENOP in vitro (preclinical) [PMID:41106613].
- Brazil nuts (food): a rich source that adds to supplement intake [PMID:18258628] [PMID:36698500].
*** Conditions
- Type 2 diabetes or high diabetes risk: caution [PMID:29974401] [PMID:27530657].
- Hashimoto thyroiditis: TPOAb lowering is likely, clinical benefit uncertain [PMID:38243784] [PMID:23744563].
- TPOAb-positive pregnancy: possible reduction of postpartum thyroiditis (single RCT) [PMID:17284630].
- Mild active Graves orbitopathy: EUGOGO 2021 lists selenium (in selenium-deficient areas) with risk-factor control and local measures [PMID:34297684] [PMID:21591944].
- Graves hyperthyroidism: no remission benefit [PMID:41384622].
- Hemodialysis: raises selenium levels without lipid or inflammation effects [PMID:39900786].
- Critical illness: high-dose IV selenite showed no mortality benefit [PMID:27428731]. Reviews recommend repletion only for biomarker-confirmed deficiency [PMID:42681987].
*** Lab-test interference
- No assay artifact found. True physiological changes: TPOAb/TgAb and TSH down in autoimmune thyroiditis [PMID:38243784] [PMID:40898469]; plasma/erythrocyte selenium and GPx activity up [PMID:18193397] [PMID:8825978].
- Plasma selenium reflects recent supplement intake and falls quickly after stopping [PMID:18193397].

** Genotype notes
- GPX1 Pro198Leu (rs1050450): modified selenium status, GPx activity and DNA-damage responses to Brazil nuts in small trials [PMID:21208780] [PMID:26661784].
- SELENOP (SEPP1) rs3877899 and rs7579 set plasma SELENOP isoform ratios, a difference that disappears after supplementation [PMID:19453253]. They were associated with glucose and cholesterol responses to Brazil nuts [PMID:28501922].
- Cochrane notes genetic background may modify selenium-cancer relations [PMID:29376219]. MR found that genetically higher selenium did not lower prostate cancer risk [PMID:29788239].
- None of these variants is validated for dosing decisions.

** Latest research
- [2026-09] Critical-care review: no routine high-dose micronutrients; replete only proven deficiency [PMID:42681987].
- [2026-06] GO MA with TSA (5 RCTs, n=303): CAS MD -1.05. No consistent QoL, visual or palpebral benefit [PMID:42355878].
- [2026-06] Preclinical: SeMet attenuated cisplatin ototoxicity via GPX4 [PMID:41698483].
- [2026-05] Pilot RCT of selenium nanoparticles in sepsis: lower IL-6 and mortality reported [PMID:41825095].
- [2026-04] MA (3 studies, n=288): myo-inositol + selenium lowered TSH vs selenium alone (SMD -1.26) [PMID:42122912].
- [2026-02] Editorial: large effects in Hashimoto, few in Graves disease [PMID:41677203]. Same month: vitamin A suppresses SELENOP in vitro [PMID:41106613].
- [2026-01] GRASS RCT (n=430): no effect on Graves remission or QoL [PMID:41384622].
- [2025-10] GDM MA (4 RCTs): FPG MD -5.03 at 200 ug/d [PMID:41073938].
- [2025-09] Cohort MA (20 studies): mortality RR 0.87 per SD of selenium status [PMID:40690813].
- [2025-08] Hashimoto MA (21 RCTs, n=1610): TPOAb SMD -0.80 at 6 months [PMID:40898469]. Same month: skin cancer MA links selenium to SCC [PMID:40674897].
- [2025-07] Hemodialysis MA: selenium levels up, no lipid or CRP effect [PMID:39900786].
- [2025-06] KiSel-10 sex analysis: CV survival benefit, greater in women [PMID:40563319].
- [2024-12] GO MA (4 RCTs): CAS MD -1.27 at 6 months [PMID:39138905]. Lipid MA (25 RCTs): only VLDL reduced [PMID:39260819].
- [2024-11] SR of selenium as complementary cancer therapy: benefit only in deficient patients [PMID:39374898].

** Open questions
- Which baseline status (plasma selenium or SELENOP) identifies people who benefit rather than are harmed? Most trials did not stratify by status [PMID:42355878] [PMID:39374898].
- Whether lower TPOAb in Hashimoto translates into fewer cases of hypothyroidism or better QoL [PMID:23744563].
- Whether the KiSel-10 CV benefit is due to selenium, CoQ10 or both. The result needs independent replication [PMID:26624886].
- Is the T2D signal form- or sex-specific (SeMet/Se-yeast vs selenite)? [PMID:29974401]
- Whether selenium protects against cisplatin toxicity in adequately powered RCTs without reducing efficacy [PMID:16856073].
- Clinical utility of GPX1/SELENOP genotyping [PMID:29376219].
