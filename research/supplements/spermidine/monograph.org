** Identity & forms
- Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine present in foods; supplements commonly use wheat-germ extract [PMID:2792725].
** Mechanisms
- Polyamine involved in cellular growth and autophagy regulation; proposed longevity effects are mostly preclinical [PMID:2792725].

** Dosing in trials
- Human RCT studied 0.9 mg/day from wheat germ extract for 12 months [URL:https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2792725].
** Safety & adverse effects
- In trial, adverse events balanced; sample size and duration insufficient for rare or long-term risks [URL:https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2792725].
** Contraindications
- No established clinical contraindications; wheat allergy may matter for source-specific products.
** Interactions

** Latest research
- 2025 review of cognitive aging summarizes only four intervention trials and notes limited human evidence; preclinical claims should not be presented as demonstrated benefit [PMID:41098596].
