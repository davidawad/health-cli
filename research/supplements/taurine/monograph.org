** Identity & forms
- Taurine is a sulfur-containing amino sulfonic acid present in food and tissues; it is not incorporated into proteins [PMID:41369612].
** Mechanisms
- Functions in bile salt conjugation, osmoregulation, calcium handling and neural signaling; clinical supplementation mechanisms remain under investigation [PMID:41369612].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials often used 1–6 g/day for several weeks; acute exercise studies used single varied doses, not a validated chronic regimen [DOI:10.1111/sms.70123].
** Safety & adverse effects
- Short trials generally tolerated; long-term high-dose safety and interactions less well characterized.
** Contraindications
- No established contraindications; caution in significant kidney disease due to limited dose safety evidence.
** Interactions
- Potential additive BP reduction with antihypertensives is plausible; evidence insufficient for defined interaction.

** Latest research
- 2025-08: 2025 acute-dose review pooled 23 RCTs (308 participants), effect g=0.25; task-specific effects and heterogeneity mean clinical significance uncertain [DOI:10.1111/sms.70123].
