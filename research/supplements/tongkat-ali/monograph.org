** Identity & forms
- Tongkat ali is root of Eurycoma longifolia Jack, sold as powders and water extracts standardized variably to eurycomanone; products are standardized variably [PMID:36013514].

** Mechanisms
- Quassinoids including eurycomanone are proposed to affect steroidogenesis and stress pathways; mechanism and human exposure-response remain uncertain [PMID:36013514].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials have used standardized extracts at roughly 100–600 mg/day, often for several weeks; formulation and standardization vary and there is no established optimal regimen [PMID:36013514].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Short-term trials report few serious events but are underpowered for uncommon harms. long-term safety is not characterized in the small trial literature [PMID:36013514].

** Contraindications
- Pregnancy/lactation and hormone-sensitive disease safety are not established. data in pregnancy/lactation and hormone-sensitive conditions are inadequate [PMID:36013514].

** Interactions
- Drugs, supplements, conditions, and relevant laboratory interference are summarized in the structured interaction object. Potential additive androgen effects with testosterone therapy are plausible; no robust interaction trials. Monitor concern around glucose/BP changes remains uncertain [PMID:36013514]. No genotype-specific claim is made because no directly relevant pharmacogenomic source was identified.

** Genotype notes
- No directly relevant, clinically validated pharmacogenomic/nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed.

** Latest research
- No qualifying large, definitive human trial published in the last 24 months was identified in the searched literature; 2022 meta-analysis remains the main RCT synthesis [PMID:36013514].

** Open questions
- Independent trials must standardize extracts, measure clinically meaningful symptoms and hormone changes, and monitor hepatic/reproductive safety [PMID:36013514].
