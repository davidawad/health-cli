** Identity & forms
- Urolithin A is represented by these commonly studied or marketed forms: UA, mitophagy-associated gut microbial metabolite. Clinical products and formulations can differ in composition and bioavailability; this limits cross-trial comparisons [PMID:39002645, PMID:35584623].

** Mechanisms
- Proposed mechanisms are outcome- and formulation-dependent; mechanistic plausibility does not establish clinical efficacy. Human evidence is summarized below [PMID:39002645, PMID:35584623].

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RCTs report changes in mitochondrial/immune biomarkers and selected muscle endurance measures, but strength, walking performance and clinically meaningful outcomes remain uncertain [PMID:35584623]. | Systematic review/meta-analysis or human trial synthesis | Mixed/possible benefit; no established clinical disease prevention | See evidence summary below; pooled estimates vary by outcome and population | low | PMID:39002645, PMID:35584623 |

- 2024 systematic review of human studies found early evidence, small trial base and no evidence for lifespan extension; functional benefits are not consistent across endpoints [PMID:39002645].
- RCTs report changes in mitochondrial/immune biomarkers and selected muscle endurance measures, but strength, walking performance and clinically meaningful outcomes remain uncertain [PMID:35584623].

** Dosing in trials
- Human RCTs commonly use 500–1,000 mg/day for 4 weeks to 4 months [PMID:35584623].

** Safety & adverse effects
- Short-term RCTs generally found good tolerability; long-term and special-population safety are not established [PMID:35584623].

** Contraindications
- Evidence insufficient for pregnancy/lactation, children, or severe hepatic/renal impairment [PMID:39002645].

** Interactions
- No well-established clinical drug interactions or laboratory assay interference identified in the human trial reviews [PMID:39002645].
- No genotype-specific clinical claim is made because no qualifying pharmacogenomic source was identified for this research set.

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source identified for a clinically useful genotype-specific note.

** Latest research
- 2024–2026: 2024 systematic review describes human healthspan evidence as preliminary and calls for larger/longer trials [PMID:39002645].

** Open questions
- Larger, preregistered, adequately powered human trials with standardized, chemically characterized products and longer follow-up are needed to establish clinically important benefits and characterize uncommon harms.
