** Identity & forms
- Vitamin B6 comprises pyridoxine, pyridoxal and pyridoxamine and their phosphorylated forms; pyridoxal-5-phosphate (PLP) is the principal active coenzyme in blood. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/]

** Mechanisms
- PLP is a coenzyme for amino acid metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, hemoglobin synthesis and glycogenolysis. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/]

** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deficiency | Replacement evidence | benefit | Corrects deficiency and associated clinical manifestations | high | URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/ |
| Nausea in pregnancy | RCTs/guideline evidence | modest benefit | Pyridoxine sometimes reduces nausea; combination with doxylamine has stronger evidence | moderate | URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/ |

** Dosing in trials
- RDA is 1.3–1.7 mg/day in adults by age/sex. Preeclampsia/nausea and other trials use indication-specific doses. EFSA established adult UL 12 mg/day; U.S. FNB UL differs (100 mg/day). [PMID:37207271, URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/]

** Safety & adverse effects
- Chronic supplemental excess causes sensory peripheral neuropathy; risk is dose- and duration-related and cases have been reported at relatively modest doses. Avoid stacking multivitamins/energy products. [PMID:37207271]

** Contraindications
- Avoid prolonged high-dose pyridoxine absent a specific indication; caution with existing neuropathy and levodopa without carbidopa. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/]

** Interactions
- Levodopa without carbidopa: pyridoxine can accelerate peripheral levodopa decarboxylation. Isoniazid, cycloserine, hydralazine and penicillamine can lower B6; some antiepileptics affect status. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB6-HealthProfessional/]

** Latest research (last 24 months)
- EFSA 2023 systematic assessment set adult UL at 12 mg/day based on neuropathy; Australia’s 2025 regulatory notice described continued adverse event monitoring for B6 neuropathy. [PMID:37207271]

** Open questions
- Clarify susceptible individuals, low-dose duration thresholds, and whether PLP formulations differ meaningfully in neurotoxicity risk.
