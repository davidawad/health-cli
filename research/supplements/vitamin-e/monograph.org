** Identity & forms
- Vitamin E denotes eight fat-soluble tocopherol/tocotrienol compounds; alpha-tocopherol is the form maintained in human plasma. Supplements use natural RRR- or synthetic all-rac-alpha-tocopherol. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/]

** Mechanisms
- Proposed biological activity and the best-established physiological context are described above; clinical mechanisms remain uncertain where outcome evidence is limited. ["URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/"]

** Dosing in trials
- Trials span 23–800 IU/day, commonly 400 IU/day; take with food containing fat for absorption. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/]

** Safety & adverse effects
- High supplemental doses may cause hemorrhagic stroke/bleeding; adult supplemental UL 1,000 mg/day. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/]

** Contraindications
- Hemorrhagic disorders and perioperative use warrant caution because of anticoagulant effects. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/]

** Interactions
- Warfarin/antiplatelet drugs: increased bleeding potential; low vitamin K intake may amplify. Antioxidant combinations may blunt statin+niacin HDL response. No routine assay interference established. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/]

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source was identified in the sources reviewed; no genotype-specific claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2025–2026 literature: 2025 NHANES cohort reported dietary intake associations with CVD; observational findings do not establish benefit from supplements. [PMID:39790118]

** Open questions
- Whether findings translate into durable patient-important outcomes, optimal formulation/dose, and long-term safety remains uncertain. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminE-HealthProfessional/; PMID:25398301]
