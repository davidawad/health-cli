** Identity & forms
- Marine protein complex supplement marketed for hair thinning; formulas vary by country and product version. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Mechanisms
- Nutrients may support normal follicle cycling; no validated mechanism establishes benefit independent of correcting dietary insufficiency. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Evidence summary by outcome
| Outcome | Best evidence type | Direction | Effect size | GRADE-style certainty | Citations |
|---------+--------------------+-----------+-------------+-----------------------+-----------|
| Subclinical hair thinning | Two small manufacturer-associated placebo-controlled trials reported improved hair counts/density over 90-180 days; independent replication and comparative efficacy are lacking | Possible / uncertain | Two small manufacturer-associated placebo-controlled trials reported improved hair counts/density over 90-180 days; independent replication and comparative efficacy are lacking. | low | [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633] |
** Dosing in trials
- Twice daily in the cited trials, for 3-6 months; trial formulation is not necessarily current retail formulation. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Safety & adverse effects
- GI symptoms and allergy are possible; marine/fish/shellfish components create allergy concern. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Contraindications
- Avoid with fish or shellfish allergy; pregnancy/lactation safety is not established. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Interactions
- Potential duplicated nutrients with multivitamins; no well-established drug interactions. Marine ingredient sourcing/formula matters. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Genotype notes
- No qualifying nutrigenomic evidence located.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: No robust independent human RCT or systematic review published in the last 24 months was identified in this search. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
** Open questions
- Product composition, active component, and durability after discontinuation remain uncertain. [PMID:26903744] [PMID:27506633]
