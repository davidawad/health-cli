** Identity & forms
- Combination label denotes red yeast rice (Monascus-fermented rice) plus garlic; product potency depends on monacolin K content and manufacturing. Monacolin K is chemically identical to lovastatin, and products may vary greatly in monacolin content and citrinin contamination [PMID:38794691; URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice]. Garlic supplements vary by preparation (powder, oil, aged extract) [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ Garlic-HealthProfessional/].

** Mechanisms
- Monacolin K inhibits HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme in cholesterol synthesis, as lovastatin does; garlic sulfur compounds may affect vascular tone/platelet activity, but proposed mechanisms vary by garlic preparation [PMID:38794691; URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/garlic].

** Dosing in trials
- Trials of red yeast rice commonly used products yielding roughly 3–10 mg/day monacolin K for 4–24 weeks; exact monacolin content is often undisclosed [PMID:38794691; URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice]. Garlic trial dosing varies widely by powder/extract, so a fixed combination dose cannot be generalized [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Garlic-HealthProfessional/].

** Safety & adverse effects
- RYR can produce statin-like myopathy, liver injury and drug interactions; citrinin contamination can injure kidneys. FDA considers products with added/enhanced lovastatin not lawful dietary supplements in the US [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice]. Garlic can cause odor, reflux/GI upset and may increase bleeding risk [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Garlic-HealthProfessional/].

** Contraindications
- Avoid during pregnancy/breastfeeding and with active liver disease or prior statin-associated severe muscle injury; avoid uncharacterized products due variable monacolin and citrinin content [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice]. Garlic hypersensitivity is a contraindication.

** Interactions
- RYR has lovastatin-like interactions: CYP3A4 inhibitors, cyclosporine, gemfibrozil and other statins may increase myopathy risk; grapefruit may increase exposure [URL:https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice]. Garlic may increase bleeding with anticoagulants/antiplatelets and may lower saquinavir exposure [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Garlic-HealthProfessional/].

** Genotype notes
- No cited pharmacogenomic or nutrigenomic source specific to Yeast Rice + Garlic was identified in this search; no genotype claim is made.

** Latest research
- 2024 meta-analysis of 14 double-blind trials (4–24 weeks) estimated LDL-C reduction of 35.82 mg/dL, but included heterogeneous products and did not assure comparable monacolin content [PMID:38794691]. 2024 review of Xuezhikang describes this standardized extract as pharmacologically distinct from generic retail RYR products [PMID:39525586].

** Open questions
- Larger, better reported human studies with standardized products, clinically meaningful endpoints and longer follow-up are needed; current gaps are noted above.
