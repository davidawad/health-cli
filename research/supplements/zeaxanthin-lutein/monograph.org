** Identity & forms
- Dietary carotenoid pigments concentrated in macula; commonly combined at 10 mg lutein/2 mg zeaxanthin in AREDS2. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Mechanisms
- Macular pigment absorbs short-wavelength light and has antioxidant activity; mechanistic plausibility does not establish benefit for people without AMD. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]

** Dosing in trials
- AREDS2 used lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg daily with background AREDS formulation; take with food containing fat for absorption. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Generally tolerated in trials; rare skin yellowing at high intake; long-term data beyond trial durations limited. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Contraindications
- No established absolute contraindication; smokers/former smokers should avoid beta-carotene-containing alternatives (not L/Z itself). [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Interactions
- No established clinically important drug interactions. High-dose carotenoids can compete with other carotenoids for absorption (uncertain clinical importance). [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Genotype notes
- No validated genotype-based selection; AREDS2 genotype subgroup claims are not sufficient for routine nutrigenomic recommendations.
** Latest research
- 2026-10-13 search cutoff: 2023 Cochrane update on antioxidant supplements for AMD progression included L/Z evidence and judged isolated effects uncertain [PMID:10498493]. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
** Open questions
- Whether L/Z alone prevents AMD or adds benefit beyond a complete AREDS2 regimen is unresolved. [PMID:23644932] [PMID:24310343] [PMID:10498493]
