** Identity & forms
- Essential trace element sold as salts; labels should distinguish elemental zinc from salt mass. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]
** Mechanisms
- Cofactor for hundreds of enzymes and transcription factors; supports nucleic-acid/protein synthesis, immunity, and wound healing. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]

** Dosing in trials
- Trial doses vary by indication. AREDS2 used 25 or 80 mg elemental zinc/day with copper; cold trials often use lozenges, not chronic high-dose tablets. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]
** Safety & adverse effects
- Nausea, vomiting, metallic taste; prolonged high doses impair copper absorption and can cause anemia/neutropenia. Adult UL is 40 mg/day total intake. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]
** Contraindications
- Wilson disease treatment is a supervised indication; avoid chronic excess and account for copper status. [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]
** Interactions
- Quinolone/tetracycline antibiotics and penicillamine absorption reduced when co-administered; thiazides increase urinary zinc loss. High intake reduces copper absorption. [URL:https://ods.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [URL:https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/] [PMID:23644932]
** Genotype notes
- No genotype note; no cited pharmacogenomic evidence supporting dose selection.
** Latest research
